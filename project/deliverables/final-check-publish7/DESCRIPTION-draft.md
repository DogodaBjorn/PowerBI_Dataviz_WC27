## Lines to add if there is room (each answers a question a reviewer asked)

- **The two measures.** NPV through 2032 at 8%, in 2025 dollars, is the challenge's own yardstick. It counts nothing
  after 2032, so every move also shows what it earns a year once live.
- **Why A and not B.** The alternative with the breakfast upgrade instead of the kiosks earns about $0.5M more a year,
  but less on its cost (34.0% against 37.5%) and less through 2032, and it loses more in the Low case.
- **The whole plan.** If the 2028 gate releases both tranches, the plan spends $51.3M of the $60M and earns about $7.5M
  a year by 2032; $8.7M is not needed and stays with the group.
- **Why the refresh keeps a tranche.** It does not pay back by 2032 and earns just under our 8%. It stays at the gate
  because it is the one move that answers the fading welcome in the oldest hotels, and the data cannot price what
  waiting costs there. It goes ahead only with a case beyond 2032.

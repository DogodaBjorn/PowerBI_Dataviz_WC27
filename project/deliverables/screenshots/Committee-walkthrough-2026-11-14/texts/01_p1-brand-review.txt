44 of 50 hotels; 6 have no renovation date.
Since 2018 Goodnight Inn spent $1.0M per hotel on renovation; the other brands spent $5.9M to $7.9M. None has started since 2020. Older hotels lose guest loyalty before they lose rooms: an association, not proof of cause.
Commissions took 1.0 of those points and running costs 1.8 (staff rose from 26.9% to 28.3% of revenue). Room rates rose 2.7%, in line with the other brands, so no price premium absorbs it.
Booked direct, the guest pays $2.25 more and we keep $17.47 more. Nothing rewards the guest for booking direct; member rates change that. At the 2021 channel mix, 2025 commissions would be $4.8M lower.

My read

If the aim is lowest risk with the D1 sketch you already have, option 1 is ready. If the aim is winning, option 2 does more for the two criteria the canon audit scored weakest: creativity, where it gives judges a brand idea they can repeat, and accessibility, through better color-blind separation and fonts that don't depend on Power BI web fonts. It costs a re-skin, not a rebuild. The two can also be combined: option 2's door-hanger headlines and state chips work inside option 1's palette, while option 2's fonts and blue-orange pair would close option 1's two measured gaps.

Choose by naming the kit (or the parts); activation is one line in project/project.yaml, and theme-from-brand-kit compiles the theme at stage 5.

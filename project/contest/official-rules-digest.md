# Official Rules — digest for the build

Source: *Power BI Data Visualization World Championships — ATL 2027, Official Rules*
(`official-rules-ATL-2027.pdf`, 5 pages, received 2026-11-05). Section numbers refer to
the PDF. The page, visual and data rules of the challenge itself are **not** in the rules;
they come from the Round 1 blog post and the starter file's hidden pages.

## Dates (§3) — all times Pacific

| Round | Opens 9:00 a.m. PT | Closes 11:59 p.m. PT |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | November 4, 2026 | **November 18, 2026** (= November 19, 08:59 CET) |
| Round 2 | November 25, 2026 | December 9, 2026 |
| Live finale at FabCon Atlanta | March 8–12, 2027 | |

Each round is entered separately; non-winning entries do not carry over. Two winners per
round; only Round 1–2 winners reach the finale. Preliminary winners are announced after
December 15 (§8).

## Who may enter (§4)

18 or older (age of majority or guardian consent); not Microsoft employees, affiliates or
agencies, nor their households; **government and public-sector employees are not
eligible**; void in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Sudan, Syria, Crimea, Russia.

## How to enter (§5)

Challenge details at aka.ms/pbidvwc26; submit on the Fabric Community website. **One
entry per round**, three across the contest. Multiple accounts void entries. "Use of any
automated system to participate is prohibited" — this concerns entry mechanics; the
starter file explicitly allows AI-assisted design tools.

## What the entry must be (§6)

Original work; not a winner in another contest; all consents and licences obtained for
anything submitted. Microsoft trademarks, logos and designs may be used under a limited
licence for the submission. No content that is obscene, offensive, violent, defamatory,
disparaging, illegal, that promotes alcohol, illegal drugs, tobacco or a political agenda,
or that reflects negatively on Microsoft's goodwill.

Build consequences: built-in fonts and our own images and icons only (already required
for Publish-to-Web); no third-party imagery or fonts whose licence does not permit
Microsoft's reuse; keep bar or alcohol imagery out of the chrome.

## What Microsoft may do with it (§7)

No ownership claim, but an irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use the entry
for any purpose, including marketing; the entry may be posted publicly; no compensation.
Nothing client-identifiable in the report (the dataset is fictional; the rule is met).

## Judging (§8)

Four criteria in the rules: **Insightfulness, Visual Effectiveness, Creativity and
Innovation, Accessibility**. A tie is broken on the same criteria. The starter file's
Judging Criteria page lists a fifth, **Analytical Quality**; the build designs for all
five. Finalists present on-site (or virtually) and are judged on the same criteria for
one World Champion. Winners are notified within seven days of judging.

## Prizes (§9)

Per round winner: conference admission and three nights' hotel in Atlanta (ARV $2,549);
airfare and other travel costs are the winner's own; virtual presentation allowed (no
travel prize then). Grand prize: title and trophy. One prize package per person overall.

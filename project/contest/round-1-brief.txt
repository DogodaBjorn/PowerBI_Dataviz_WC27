The Power BI Dataviz World Championships are BACK! ���� We're kicking things off with a challenge that puts your analytical skills, creativity, and storytelling to the test. This time, you're not just building a report - you're making the decisions!
Meet Pillow & Key Group
Pillow & Key Group is a fictional hospitality company with 165 hotels across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The company operates four brands, each with its own customers, challenges, and opportunities.

The Board of Directors is preparing its next five-year investment strategy. But there's a catch: there isn't enough money to fund everything. That's where you come in!
���� Your Challenge
Choose one brand and build a Power BI report that helps the Board make smart investment decisions. Your report should answer two big questions:
1. The Brand Review
Where does your brand stand today?
Explore the historical data to uncover what matters most. What's working? What's not? Where are the biggest opportunities?
The Board doesn't need every number. They need the insights that will help them make decisions.
2. The Investment Strategy
Where should the money go, and why?
Turn your findings into a compelling investment proposal.
Your strategy must:
Recommend at least three investment initiatives.
Include at least one initiative focused on future growth.
Stay within your brand's budget.
Demonstrate expected financial and operational impact through 2032.
Explain your choices, risks, and trade-offs.
Remember, there's no single right answer. We're looking for thoughtful analysis, clear recommendations, and a story that makes the Board pay attention!

Two bars, four parameters, three refusals.

Everything on the previous four pages rests on four disclosed choices. Where the data cannot support a claim, this report declines to make one.

The two bars
The money bar

Budget and revenue both at or above $10,000. Below that floor a ratio is arithmetic noise: an $800 budget returning $8,000 is not a 10× success, it is a rounding error with ambition.

The evidence bar

At least 100 votes. Below m = 100 an average rating is a rumour; at or above it the weighted rating pulls toward the prior C = 6.96 in proportion to the evidence a title has gathered.

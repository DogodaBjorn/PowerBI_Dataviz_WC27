# AxisX hooks: community leaders and members

Built with: `viral-hooks` (Vyral, archetypes, three-layer hook, anti-patterns) and `hook-generator` (2-line, 40-character format for founder posts). Checked against `regels.md`: English, no em-dashes, no diagnose/cure/treat/prescribe, no health-outcome promise, beta stated honestly, incentive numbers left as placeholders until decided.

## 0. Why the current line is not a hook

"From feeling lost in your own body to feeling in control again."

- **Generic transformation.** Any wellness app (BetterMe, Balance, a coach) can say it. No specific noun, nothing only AxisX does.
- **No mechanism.** It hides the two things that are actually different: one personal system instead of five scattered places, and a report you take to your doctor after about two weeks.
- **Wrong audience for the strategy.** It talks to an individual woman in a feed. Our route is ABM on communities: the first reader is a moderator, a group admin or a newsletter owner, and they are asking "why should I risk my members' trust on you?"
- **Promise risk.** "In control again" edges toward an outcome claim. Keep it as a positioning line on the landing page, not as the opener.

So: two hook sets. One for the gatekeeper, one for the members the gatekeeper lets us talk to.

## 1. Hooks for community leaders (direct outreach)

What every leader gets, so the hook is not empty: (a) reserved Founding places for their members, (b) an anonymised, theme-level summary of what their community asks about, shared with them first, (c) a founder Q&A on their terms, (d) no obligation to endorse any claim. The hook opens the door; the give is in line 2 or 3.

| Leader type | Channel | Subject / first line | Opening (founder voice) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subreddit moderator (r/Perimenopause, r/Menopause) | Modmail | **Founder Q&A request: you set the rules** | "I'm building a perimenopause app in closed beta and I won't post in your sub without your yes. If you'd allow a Q&A, you decide the format, the timing and whether a link appears at all." |
| Facebook group / community founder (Latte Lounge type) | Email or DM to admin | **[50] Founding places, held for your members** | "Your members already help each other make sense of perimenopause. I'd like to give [50] of them first access to a tool that keeps it all in one place, and give you a say in what we build next." |
| Newsletter writer / podcast host | Email | **What 60 women told me before I wrote any code** | "I interviewed 60 women in midlife before building AxisX. Three things came up again and again: losing grip on their own day, advice scattered everywhere, and putting themselves last. Your readers will recognise all three." |
| Menopause coach, nurse, practitioner | Email / LinkedIn | **A two-week summary your clients can take to their GP** | "Your clients arrive with notes on their phone, screenshots and a feeling. AxisX turns two weeks of what they log into one page they can bring to their doctor. You don't have to endorse anything; I'd value your eye on whether it's useful." |
| Peanut group host / WhatsApp circle lead | In-app message (if rules allow) | **Your group sees it first. Nobody gets sold to.** | "We're opening a small founding group before launch. I'd rather start with circles like yours, where women are honest, than with ads." |

### Founder LinkedIn posts aimed at leaders (hook-generator format, max 40 characters per line)

```
I won't post in your group uninvited.      (37)
So I'm asking the moderator first.         (34)

I interviewed 60 women before building.    (39)
They asked for order, not more advice.     (37)

I'm looking for 500 women, not users.      (37)
Co-builders for the app they wanted.       (36)
```
Check with the founder: "order, not more advice" is our reading of "fragmented information" in the brief, not a quote.

## 2. Hooks for members, per persona (only where a moderator said yes)

All personas are inside the brief's segment: 39+, already recognises perimenopause, wants support. Variety is deliberate: the founder wants different life situations in the 500. Each hook gets one tag in the URL so we can read application, onboarding and D7 per persona.

### P1. "The month everything changed" (brief's hottest subsegment, most willing to pay)
- **Investigator:** "Sleep, mood, memory, cycle. All different in the same month, and none of your notes connect."
  On-screen: *4 changes. 0 connections.* Visual: phone screen with notes app, calendar, screenshots, a sleep app, swiped through fast.
- **Teacher:** "You don't need another article tonight. You need one place where it all adds up."
  On-screen: *Stop collecting. Start connecting.*

### P2. "The planner" (gradual onset, 7 to 10 years ahead, "an app for women who think")
- **Contrarian:** "You track your runs, your sleep and your money. Your hormones get a notes app."
  On-screen: *Tracked everything. Except this.*
- **Fortuneteller:** "This could last years. Start the record now, while it's still manageable."

### P3. "Three GP visits and still 'let's wait and see'" (first value moment: the two-week report)
- **Experimenter:** "Two weeks of logging. One page. Walk into your appointment with evidence, not 'I just don't feel like me'."
  On-screen: *2 weeks. 1 page. For your GP.* Visual: hand sliding a single printed page across a desk.
- **Contrarian:** "Your doctor has ten minutes. Make them count."
  Source for the context (deck, not ad): 24% of US women who sought treatment "had to push" to be taken seriously (Gallup, Jul 2026).

### P4. "Last on her own list" (brief: women don't admit how deep it goes)
- **Admission:** "You've said 'I'm fine' for months. Here's a place to write down what isn't."
- **Reframe:** "You'd never let someone you love go a year like this without a plan."

### P5. "The sceptic" (15 to 20% of interviewees sceptical of AI)
- **Contrarian:** "Most menopause apps give you content. This one gives you a record."
- **Plain promise:** "No chatbot pep talk. No diagnosis. A system built from what you tell it, that you can take to your doctor."

### Founding 500 umbrella (works for every persona, carries the exclusivity)
- "We're looking for 500 women to build the app they wish existed. Not testers. Co-builders."
- "Closed beta, [incentive], and a direct line to the founder. In return: tell us what's missing."

## 3. Referral hooks (member to friend, no medical label)

The friend must not be labelled. The inviter invites to a group, not to a diagnosis.
- "I'm one of 500 women helping build an app for midlife health. I can bring one friend. Want the spot?"
- "I've got a founding invite. Thought of you first."

---

## 4. What we test, and on what

- **Leaders:** reply rate and "yes" rate per leader type, founder hours per yes. A hook that gets replies but no permission has failed.
- **Members:** do not judge on clicks or CTR. Judge on quiz completed → application → full onboarding → D7 open, per persona tag. Minimum read: 30 onboardings per persona before calling a winner.
- **Kill:** a persona hook with onboarding but D7 clearly below the others gets cut, even if it pulls the most applications.
- Three-layer check for every video version: works muted, first frame shows the conflict, text sharpens rather than repeats.

## 5. Rejected
- "Is this perimenopause?" (suggested in our own audience research note): targets women who don't recognise it yet, which the brief puts out of scope for the first 500.
- Clickbait rules from `hook-generator` (curiosity gap, "you won't believe"): kept the format, dropped the tactic. Trust is the scarce resource with moderators.
- Any line with "fix", "heal", "balance your hormones": outcome claims the product cannot back.

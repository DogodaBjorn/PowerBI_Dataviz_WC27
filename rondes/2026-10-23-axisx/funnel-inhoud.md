## 3. Quiz = qualification (8 screens, about 2 minutes)
Selection principle: recognition + already searching + sees value in the method + can try it now. NOT symptom severity. No diagnosis required (perimenopause is recognised clinically from symptoms; NICE NG23).
| # | Question | Answers | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Tell it like you'd tell a friend: what's changed lately?" | free text | Product-native start; feeds the system preview. Stored as health data, never sent to ad pixels |
| 1 | Age | under 39 / 39-44 / 45-49 / 50-55 / 56+ | Under 39: later list |
| 2 | "Which fits you best?" | "I'm fairly sure perimenopause has started" / "I suspect it has" / "I'm not sure it's that" | Third answer: later list (brief: not in scope now) |
| 3 | "In the last 3 months, have you...?" (multi) | talked to a GP or nurse · tracked it somewhere · joined a group or forum · read up on it · nothing yet | Behaviour, not intention: strongest fit signal |
| 4 | "What would help most right now?" | see patterns · prepare for my doctor · build habits that stick · feel less alone in it | Shapes preview; persona tag |
| 5 | "What does your life look like?" (multi) | work full time · care for children · care for parents · shift work · lots of travel · other | Quota for variety |
| 6 | "Could you spend 2 minutes a day for the next 14 days?" | yes / most days / not right now | "Not right now": later list |
| 7 | "Do you use an iPhone?" | yes / no | No: Android waitlist |
Result screen: preview of HER system (3 things to track, 2 habits, 1 reminder time, "your doctor page on day 14"), generated by AI from answers 0 and 4. Disclaimer under it. No score (Flo owns "Perimenopause Score").

## 5. Selection model: three outcomes
| Outcome | Rule (start weights, recalibrated) |
|---|---|
| Invite now (cohort 1) | 39+, recognises or suspects, at least 1 behaviour in Q3, yes to 2 min/day, iPhone, quota not full |
| Cohort 2 test | Lower fit (e.g. no Q3 behaviour, "maybe" on feedback) plus a random 10-15% sample of all eligible applicants: comparison group for plan B |
| Later | Under 39, "not sure it's that", no time now, no iPhone (Android waitlist) |
Recalibration after the first 50-100 onboarded: compare quiz answers with Active founder D7 and D7 open; drop questions that don't predict, raise weight of those that do.
Volume: 500 approved is not 500 onboarded. Invite to onboarding complete is an assumption of ~70% (to measure) so about 715 invites for 500 onboarded, then 60% D7 = 300.

## 7. Visual system (one recognisable asset)
- **System card** (distinctive asset, in every piece): one card per real member (consented, anonymised), showing her system: e.g. "07:00 tablet, 1 h before food · 12:00 protein · 17:00 how do I feel?" Same layout, colours and type every time. AI renders the card from consented data; no faces generated.
- **Doctor page** mock: generic, no real data, "2 weeks. 1 page." as visual promise.
- **Video**: phone-shot talking head, captions, full sentence on the first frame (format of the long runners in the category: Flo 202-215 days, BioID 214-435 days, induik 6).
- **Series "No two systems alike"**: carousel of system cards from members, the opposite of the founder's synthetic-persona idea.
- AI's role in visuals: card rendering, captions, cutting 8 openings, resizing. Never people, never voices.

# Deep dive 6: Ad Library run length and format (competitors)

Compiled 2026-10-23. Reference date for "days running": 2026-10-23.
Tools: (A) Meta Ad Library MCP `ads_library_search` (claude_ai_Meta_Ad, read-only); (B) Web2Wave Trends API (`/creatives/brands`, `/creatives/brands/{id}/analytics`, `/creatives/ads`, project_id dogoda, read-only); (C) own visual check of thumbnails downloaded from the Web2Wave creative store (first frame only for video). Public Ad Library pages via WebFetch returned HTTP 403, so no fallback from there.

## Answer first
1. The only long-running "winner" creatives in the perimenopause space are first-person, phone-shot talking-head videos with a big text overlay: a real-looking woman says what she felt and what helped (Flo "Perimenopause Score" video ran 211 days on 292 ad copies; BioID clinic video ran 435 days on 1,880 copies).
2. The winning hook is a self-assessment or validation moment ("My Perimenopause Score showed me...", "My GP told me I was overreacting"), followed by a low-friction action (free score, GBP 25 consult). This is close to AxisX's "your situation, your system, report for your doctor" promise.
3. Big brands (Flo, Midi) run hundreds of fresh variants and rotate weekly, so a single long-runner is rare; what survives is the format and the hook, not the individual ad. Supplement and "named woman" advertorial pages churn even faster (most started in the last 0-5 days).

## Patterns
1. Format that lasts: vertical UGC talking head plus a full-sentence text overlay in the first frame. The four longest-running creatives we could see (Flo 202 to 215 days, BioID 214 and 435 days) all fit this, with one exception: Flo's illustrated "crying at the mirror" story video. Static images last too (Flo Score statics 87 to 132 days, Healthtime advertorial 216 days) but at far fewer copies, except advertorial pages.
2. Hook that lasts: validation plus self-measurement. "Why does no one talk about...", "My GP told me I was overreacting", then a score, quiz or consult as the next step. No long-runner leads with features or with "natural".
3. Flo scale and churn (Web2Wave brand analytics): 10,320 ads, 2,775 creatives since Oct 2025, 817 active vs 1,958 ended; 71.6% video, 28.4% image; heavy launches in Aug to Oct 2026 (646, 1,559, 1,059 new ads per month). Meta MCP shows 799 active ads on that page, the newest 50 all started 22 to 23 Oct. Most of Flo's traffic now goes to a web landing (psc.floapp.io, 1,750 ads) next to the App Store (5,840) and Play (842), a sign of web-to-app funnels.
4. Midi rotates fast: 324 active ads (MCP), none older than about 14 days in what we could see. They mix clinician authority, symptom checklists, a disclosed creator partner ad and new product lines (GLP-1, omega-3). Insurance-backed CAC allows this volume; AxisX cannot copy the volume, only the hooks.
5. App competitors are almost absent: anna app has 3 active ads (all started today). Balance, Stella, Peppy, Clue, Elektra, Caria, Evernow, Joii showed no ads in our searches. The paid space is held by Flo, clinics (Midi, BioID) and supplement or advertorial pages.
6. All Flo and BioID winners run on all Meta placements including Audience Network and Threads (Web2Wave data). Placement choice is not the edge; creative is.

So what for AxisX: test a real woman, phone-shot, talking about one specific moment, with a first-frame overlay sentence and the "your personal system / report for your doctor" demo as the payoff (Flo's Score is the nearest analogue). Avoid the advertorial "named doctor / named woman" look: it is the supplement playground and churns in days.

## Caveats
- Meta MCP returns start date, page, link title and currency only: no format, no platforms, no visuals, no number of versions. It sorts newest first and caps at 50, so for big pages (Flo 799, Midi 324, BioID 678 active) we cannot see the oldest active ads; "oldest seen" is a lower bound, not the true maximum.
- Web2Wave lifespan is first_seen to last_seen in its own crawl. Several items are flagged is_active but last_seen is July 2026, so treat lifespan as observed run length, not proof the ad is live today. Web2Wave covers Flo, BioID and some advertisers, not Midi, Balance, anna app or most named-woman pages.
- Ad id vs creative id: Web2Wave numbers like 1550873613124890 are creative or media ids; one creative can sit in hundreds of ads (ad_count). They may not open as Ad Library ids.
- Visuals: described only for 8 thumbnails we actually opened (first frame of video). Everything else says "not returned" or "not checked". We did not watch full videos.
- Long run length is a proxy for performance, not proof. Big advertisers may keep ads running for reasons other than results; small pages may stop good ads for budget reasons.
- Peppy's only ads found are from 2021 via an employer page, which fits its B2B model; absence of Balance and others may be a naming issue (page not searched by id).
- Ad Library public pages via WebFetch returned HTTP 403, so no fallback check of dates, platforms or versions there.

## So what for the $50k plan

- Plan Meta at a Health & Wellness CPM of ~$20 (range $15-25, Q4 higher; UK Dec spike). At $20 CPM and 1.5-2.7% CTR, cost per landing click is roughly $0.75-1.35. Use that as the step-1 cost anchor, not the all-industry $0.70.
- Women cost more to reach (~40-50% higher CPM than men per Lebesgue). No public 39+ women CPM exists, so measure it in week 1 by age bucket and report it as our own data.
- Biggest structural risk: if Meta classifies AxisX (perimenopause) as health & wellness, Lead/Purchase/CompleteRegistration may be blocked and UTMs stripped. Check Events Manager data source category on day 0. Fallback: optimize to a neutral custom event (e.g. "quiz_complete" named without health terms), measure step costs server-side/web2wave, not in Meta. This is also why "cost per completed onboarding per step" must come from our own funnel data.
- Step funnel to model (Adapty benchmark mid-points): click 100 -> quiz start 50 -> quiz complete 30 -> paywall view 23 -> paid/application 0.5-1.2 (2-5% of paywall). For an invite/application funnel without payment, the application step will be far higher than 2-5%; treat Adapty's paywall rate as paid-only.
- Example cost per completed quiz at $1 per click and 50% x 60%: ~$3.30. Each step multiplies; show the table per step so investors see where cost rises.
- Saturation evidence is practitioner-level only. Design the test to produce our own curve: 3-4 spend tiers (e.g. $50, $150, $400/day) and watch marginal cost per onboarding plus 7-day frequency (>3-4 = saturation flag).
- Diversify to cheaper reach for 10x: Reddit CPM ~$3-15, TikTok ~$5-13 (low confidence). Google Search for "perimenopause" CPC is unknown; pull from Keyword Planner before budgeting.
- Creators: micro creators $500-2,500 per post (Later 2026); with whitelisting +30-50%. For $50k, a creator line of ~$5-8k buys roughly 4-10 micro posts plus usage rights for paid.
- Downstream value: H&F trial->paid 39.9% median, 67% of H&F subs are yearly, refunds 4.7%, D30 retention 3-8%. Payback case must lean on annual plans and longer trials (17-32 day trials 45.7% vs 26.8%).
- Gaps (say so in the hand-in): no public menopause CPC, no 39+ women CPM, no primary onboarding-completion benchmark, no controlled Meta diminishing-returns study.

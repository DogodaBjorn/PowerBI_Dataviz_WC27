Not ads. Not avatars. The founder asks, and AI does her research.

03Founders Circle

500 women who recognise what is happening and want to build the app they wish existed. Free in beta, a founding price at launch, three invites each.

The brief: 500 women onboarded, 60% open the app on day 7, $5,000 without paid media; a separate $50,000 scale test. What it is not asking: volume, women in denial, paid conversion now, or plan A times ten. Details: appendix §0 and §4.

What the Founding 500 experience · the engine

She tells her story. AI listens for intent. People decide.

01AI labels her words

Recognition, actions already taken, fit with the method, commitment, urgency, life context. Content, never writing style. A person reviews every edge case.

02Two groups

Signal group (~85%): women with the signals. Contrast group (~15%): in the segment, without them, drawn at random. The contrast group is the test.

03One ID, four events

Her email links quiz, invite and app login. Onboarding complete, app open, feedback given, report created. Day 7 is counted the same way every time.

Trust rules: separate consent for health data, raw text deleted after analysis, nothing with health meaning to any ad platform, no automated rejection, weekly bias check. Appendix §5.6.
GDPR art. 9 and 22: gdpr-info.eu · No diagnosis needed to qualify: NICE NG23 · TestFlight invites by email keep testers identifiable: developer.apple.com.

What the funnel teaches us for plan B

Every visit is tagged. Five numbers come out.

Visitors per woman

Per step and per source. Expected about 10 to 1. This is the cost multiplier for every paid click.

Signal vs contrast

Day 7, feedback, day-14 report for both groups. How much retention falls when selection loosens: what paid traffic will look like.

Intent profile

Which words and actions predict day 7. A day-0 quality signal for any channel.

Founding price yes

Share who lock in the launch price, no payment. The first measure of willingness to pay.

Winning hook

Judged on day 7, not clicks. Becomes the first paid creative.

Score: 500 onboarded, 60% open on day 7. Steering: feedback in week 1, the day-14 report, the founding-price yes.

Measurement plan, definitions and stop rules: appendix §4. Targets are assumptions, measured per invite code; 500 and 300 are goals, not results.

Building plan B · the $50,000 scale test

Scale only what stays under the line.

Break-even line: what a woman is worth
S1 · $12k

Calibrate four channels: Meta UK+US, paid newsletters and creators, Google Search, Reddit. Find the price per onboarded woman.

S2 · $15k

Triple daily spend on the best two. Watch cost per day-7 active and the share of signal-group applicants.

S3 · $18k

The best channel at ten times plan A's run-rate. Find where cost rises and what saturates: audience, creative or frequency.

$5k reserve for a holdout test and fresh creatives. Next stage only if cost per day-7 active and signal share hold within 25%; stop at +50%.

6/8
Building plan B · the ceiling and the honest line

What a woman is worth decides how far we can go.

The ceiling

Not the census: the US pool is not exhausted within $50k, the Nordics are. The real limit is the slice of women who recognise perimenopause and want help, and creative fatigue.

Early warning: when a channel's share of signal-group applicants drops, it is running out of the right women, days before day-7 data shows it.

The honest line

A payer is worth £85 net a year. On public benchmarks, 2.9% to 6.2% of downloads pay. At about $23 per onboarded woman, paid does not pay back in year 1.

The $50k does not buy growth. It buys the answer: do intent-selected women convert two to four times above benchmark, and where does the cost curve break?

Conversion benchmarks: RevenueCat 2026 · Populations and iOS share: ONS, US Census, Eurostat, StatCounter · Health ad restrictions: Meta, Google · Apple 15%: Small Business Program · Worked sums: appendix §1-3.

The money

Ten dollars a woman to learn. Twenty-three to find out if it scales.

$5,000
Plan A · about $10 cash per onboarded woman

Funnel and analytics, AI research and classification, content from real members, the Founders Circle space, partner reports, reserve. No paid media. Founder hours (~40) counted separately: they do not scale.

$50,000
Plan B · about $23 per onboarded woman, ~1,900 women, 3 stages

$12k calibrate, $15k triple the best two, $18k the best at ten times plan A, $5k holdout and creatives. Launched only once the five numbers from plan A are in.

The number that connects them is not a cost. It is the share of women who say yes to the founding price.

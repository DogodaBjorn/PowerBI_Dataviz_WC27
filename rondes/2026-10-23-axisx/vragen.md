# Questions to AxisX (draft, ranked by how much the answer changes the plan)

1. **Success definition.** Is the target 500 users who signed up and completed onboarding, of whom 60% are active on day 7 (so about 300 retained), or 500 retained users? And what counts as "retained" on day 7: an app open, or a core action such as logging symptoms?
   *Why: it sets the size of the top of the funnel by a factor of almost two, and an app open is easy to inflate with push notifications.*

2. **Market and language.** Which country or countries do we launch in, and in which language?
   *Why: health-ad rules, data-privacy rules, creator ecosystems and media costs differ a lot between the US, the UK and the EU.*

3. **The product today.** What does the app do in closed testing (symptom tracking, AI coach, clinician access, lab testing), what is the moment in onboarding where a user "gets it", and what will the paid tier be and cost?
   *Why: the funnel should promise exactly what the first session delivers, and plan B economics need a price.*

4. **Closed-testing limits.** How many invites can we issue per week, on which platforms (iOS, Android, both), and who decides on an "application": can we select users on fit?
   *Why: invite capacity is the hard ceiling of plan A, and selection on fit is how we hit 60% day-7 retention.*

5. **Timeline.** By when do you need the 500, and how long is the $50,000 test?
   *Why: organic and community channels compound slowly; paid moves fast.*

6. **What we already have.** Is there a waitlist, email list, social accounts, founders or clinicians willing to appear on camera, advisory board, partners (clinics, employers, communities)?
   *Why: a real expert voice beats synthetic personas on trust in women's health, and owned audiences are free under the $5,000 ceiling.*

7. **What the $5,000 may pay for.** Strictly tools, tokens and generation, or also human costs such as a clinician reviewing content, creator gifting, or referral rewards (for example free premium months)?
   *Why: the referral and loyalty design depends on what rewards we can offer.*

8. **Rules on claims and AI.** Do you have brand guidelines, a list of medical claims you can and cannot make, a medical or legal review step, and a position on disclosing AI-generated people?
   *Why: health claims and undisclosed synthetic people are the fastest way to lose trust, ad accounts and app store approval.*

9. **Measurement.** What is tracked today (onboarding completion, day-7 activity, source of each user), and can we see retention per acquisition source and per quiz answer?
   *Why: plan B rests on retention and conversion per channel and segment measured on the first 500.*

10. **Your two hypotheses.** Have you tested AI influencers or the synthetic-persona series already, and what did you see?
    *Why: we want to beat them on evidence, not opinion.*

11. **Plan B goal.** For the $50,000 test, what is the target: free users, paid conversions, or a cost per retained user? Is there a target CAC or payback period from the investor?
    *Why: "does it scale" needs a number that defines "scales".*

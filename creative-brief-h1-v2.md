# 3. Creative brief for an AI creator (v2, drop-in for hand-in-stage1.md)

Nederlandse toelichting bovenaan, daarna de negen velden in het Engels, één antwoord per veld zoals het template vraagt. Verschillen met de huidige sectie 3 staan onderaan.

---

**Which hypothesis this brief brings to life (one line)**
Hypothesis 1, "Your price. Your driver.": the split-screen fare concept that won Q3 (NG-01 static $0.97, NG-02 9:16 $1.70), rebuilt with a person, a shared ride, drivers responding and the USP in the headline (NG-07).

**Hypothesis**
Hypothesis 1, because it is the only recipe with two proofs on target (NG-01, NG-02), its visual twin NG-03 shows that format decides ($8.44 as landscape motion), and NG-07 shows that a headline naming both USPs gets the best IPM of the quarter. It serves the 35% hero message, runs on Meta and Google at once, and the petrol and Bolt updates make "a price you both agree on" the message of the moment. The person, the friend and the jumping price are deliberate additions, not a repeat of NG-01.

**Format and size**
Static. Master 9:16, 1080x1920 px, for Meta Stories and Reels placements and Google App campaigns portrait. One derived cut 1:1, 1080x1080 px, for Meta feed and Google App campaigns square. Two language variants of each size: English and Nigerian Pidgin. Four files in total, PNG plus a layered source file each, sRGB, no compression artefacts on the fare card text.

**Channel**
Meta (Instagram Stories, Reels, Facebook and Instagram feed) and Google App campaigns (Android and iOS, Nigeria). The 9:16 master is the primary Meta asset; the 1:1 cut is the primary Google asset.

**Who is in it**
Chidi, 24, Nigerian man, junior professional, Igbo-looking cast; next to him one male friend, same age, Yoruba-looking cast (most Nigerian rides are shared, and casting must be diverse). Both smart-casual: clean short-sleeve shirt or polo, chinos or dark jeans, sneakers, one small backpack. Chidi holds the phone in his right hand, thumb on the screen, half smile, eyes on the phone; the friend looks over his shoulder. Setting: a busy Lagos roadside at dusk in the style of Ikeja or Yaba: shop fronts with blurred, unreadable signage, warm street light, moving traffic as soft bokeh, no yellow buses or taxis in focus. Both on the pavement, not in a car. Real Lagos atmosphere, no stock-photo look; skin tones natural, no plastic AI sheen. The driver on the card is "Musa" (Hausa name, third group); the two other responders are "Tunde" and "Emeka".

**The inDrive moment**
The inDrive fare screen is the single largest element, at least 45% of the canvas, placed lower-left and overlapping Chidi's hand so the thumb visibly rests on it. It is readable at thumbnail size (test at 200 px wide). Content, top to bottom: label "Your offer"; the amount ₦4,100 in the largest type on the page after the headline; a horizontal slider with "–" and "+" ends and the knob two thirds to the right; a full-width green button "Agree on price"; under it three stacked driver responses "Musa · 4.9 ★ · 3 min · Accept", "Tunde · 4.8 ★ · 5 min · Accept", "Emeka · 4.7 ★ · 6 min · Accept", with Chidi's thumb on the first "Accept". Above the inDrive screen, a narrow grey band, about 12% of the canvas height, is an anonymous other app: text "Fare ₦5,600 · price updated ↑", a small lock icon, a greyed-out button, no brand, no colour, no taxi symbol, no car. The viewer therefore sees, in one glance, a price nobody can touch and a price Chidi is setting himself with his thumb, plus three drivers already saying yes to it. Both USPs are shown as actions, not as a footnote.

**Headline · visual · call to action (static)**
Headline, top of the canvas under the wordmark, PP Agrandir Bold or Poppins Bold, two lines maximum, black: EN "Your price. Your driver." / Pidgin "Na your price, na your driver." Subhead directly under it, Poppins SemiBold, black: EN "Offer your price. Drivers respond. You choose." / Pidgin "Offer your price. Drivers go answer. Na you choose." Visual: background Drive green #C1F11D; Chidi and friend right of centre from waist up, the fare card overlapping lower-left, the grey band above the card; the green, the people and the card read as one scene, not three boxes. Wordmark "inDrive" black, top-left, touching the canvas edge, never cropped or overlapped; use white only if it has to sit on the photo. Call to action: black pill button, Poppins SemiBold, white text "Set your price on inDrive", bottom-left inside the safe area. Safe area 9:16: keep all text out of the top 14% and bottom 20%, and 15% from the right edge (platform UI); 1:1: 8% margin all round. Bottom-right, Poppins Regular, small, black at 60%: "Created using AI". 1:1 cut: same elements; wordmark top-left, headline top, people right, fare card lower-left at 40% of canvas, grey band reduced to a single line above the card; subhead may drop if it collides with the card. All amounts in naira with the ₦ sign; no other currency.

**Music and voice**
None, static. If a 6 s motion cut is requested later: original Afrobeats-style instrumental, no recognisable artist, no lyrics, no Amapiano or other South African music; headline language follows the variant; no voice-over.

**Must not appear**
Words: "taxi", "commission", "negotiate" (not even as a quote), "cheap rides", "discount", "save", "faster", "verified drivers", "SOS", "share your trip" (safety features do not count as brand fit in Nigeria). Any competitor name, logo, colour scheme or recognisable app layout; a taxi-checker pattern or yellow taxi on the "other app" side; any direct comparison in words. A headline about ease, comfort, speed or safety above the price moment. Several prices on screen without the slider and the "Your offer" label (that does not count as setting the price). The fare card smaller than 45% of the canvas or the amount unreadable at 200 px. Luxury or 2022+ cars, car badges, licence plates. Real street names, shop names or landmarks, real addresses, in the photo or in any map mock. Driver details beyond first name, rating and arrival time: no surname, phone number, plate or photo of a real person; no placeholder names from the brand kit ("Mateo", "Kate"). Non-Nigerian faces; a visibly synthetic face; extra fingers or warped hands on the phone. A phone with a visible manufacturer logo, distinctive notch or camera layout (render a generic device). Dollar signs. Churches, mosques, religious buildings, alcohol, smoking. Anyone in a car without a seat belt, driver included (not applicable here: nobody is in a car). An end-card footnote or small print as the only brand-fit element. Text outside the safe areas.

## Wat v2 verandert ten opzichte van sectie 3 in hand-in-stage1.md

1. **Lay-outmaten en safe areas toegevoegd** (45% kaart, 12% grijze band, 14/20/15% safe areas voor 9:16, 8% voor 1:1, 200 px leesbaarheidstest). Zonder maten vult een AI-creator ze zelf in en wordt het fare-scherm te klein, de fout van NG-14 en NG-17.
2. **De 1:1-cut is nu beschreven.** Sectie 3 noemde hem alleen; de creator moet weten wat er wegvalt en wat niet.
3. **Casting per persoon vastgelegd** (Chidi Igbo, vriend Yoruba, chauffeur Musa Hausa) in plaats van "e.g.". Dat dekt "diverse across backgrounds" controleerbaar.
4. **Kleurcode, lettertypes per element, knopkleur en logo-regel per situatie** staan bij het element, niet alleen in het brand kit-verwijzing.
5. **Veiligheidsfeatures expliciet verboden** (verified drivers, SOS, share trip), plus AI-artefacten (handen, synthetische huid) en "text outside safe areas". Dat zijn de dingen die een creator standaard toevoegt of laat gebeuren.
6. **Leveringsspecificatie** (vier bestanden, PNG plus gelaagd, sRGB), zodat "without asking you anything" ook voor de oplevering geldt.
7. **Eén zin over de interne logica van het beeld** ("een prijs die niemand kan aanraken naast een prijs die Chidi zelf zet"), zodat de creator weet wat hij niet mag wegoptimaliseren.

Twee risico's die bewust zijn blijven staan, voor het geval de jury erover begint: de grijze band met "price updated ↑" is een anonieme vergelijking, dezelfde vorm als NG-01, geen concurrent in beeld. En twee prijzen op één canvas is toegestaan omdat de slider en "Your offer" het zet-moment tonen; Appendix B verbiedt alleen prijzen zonder dat moment.

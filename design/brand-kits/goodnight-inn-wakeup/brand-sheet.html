Goodnight Inn 3.1

Hip, modern, businesslike.

A brand ready for the Board and an investor deck. Same promise, same door hanger, same analysis; no characters, no posters. A Night header zone with the cards hanging over it gives the page its depth; the numbers carry the rest.

01The data is the image. No illustrations or textures.
02Goodnight Inn is always blue, the sisters always grey.
03Tangerine only signals: a cost, a risk, a last of four.
04One argument per page, sized by importance.
01 · The two pages

The report at 1920 × 1080, night header

The current build restyled, with its analysis unchanged. Figures come from the offline decode of the contest data and are re-derived from the model before they ship. The comps use Open Sans as a stand-in for Segoe UI and show the look, not the final type sizes (the build keeps the 12 pt floor).

02 · Depth

Depth in the frame, never in the data

No 3D charts. A flat Night zone, with the faded door hanger top right, runs from the top of the page to the horizon, 128 px below the top of the first card row. The cards start inside it and hang over the edge, with one soft shadow each and no hairline. Zone and shadows are drawn into the page background image, so no visual needs a shadow setting.

ZoneFlat Night #12233D, no gradient. Headline #F7F5EE (14.4:1), masthead and subtitle #C9D0DC (10.2:1).
CardsWhite, radius 12, no hairline, one shadow on every card: Night at 10 %, y 8, blur 24.
HangerThe faceless door hanger, faded to 6 %, tilted and cut off by the horizon, top right in the zone. Kept at your request; text over it stays at 8.3:1 or better.
OptionA gradient and a blue glow in the zone exist as "night-glow", but only with a waiver of the no-gradient rule.

Nothing below 12 pt in the report (plan 08, R4). Segoe UI is Power BI's default and ships with Windows only. Whether a Mac judge sees it under Publish-to-Web is unverified: test before stage 6, with Arial as the fallback. On this phone the page shows Open Sans, its closest open relative.

07 · From 2.0 to 3.1

What stayed, what went

Kept

  • The palette from your reference, with measured ink and mark weights.
  • One meaning per color, now stricter: blue is Goodnight Inn.
  • The door hanger as the symbol, and the numbered findings F1 to F4.
  • The analysis of the current build, figure for figure.

Parked

  • Hank the mascot, his badge and his voice: guest-facing only.
  • Riso offset, paper grain and the AI illustrations.
  • The poster wordmark with the script "Inn" and Arial Black capitals.
  • The door-hanger headlines, replaced by plain declaratives.
Goodnight Inn is a fictional brand from the PillowKey contest dataset (Power BI Dataviz World Champs 2027). Marks and layouts are original work.

The promise

A good night. A fair price.
Archetype: the Everyman (Mark and Pearson). Plain, warm, never luxury, never cute. Guests don't buy a room, they buy waking up rested and not feeling overcharged (Levitt).

Never

Raspberry as good or bad. Blue or orange as decoration. Both raspberry weights on one visual. A third live series color. The face inside a chart, or any other mascot. Moons, stars, "Zzz", navy and gold. Fonts beyond Trebuchet MS, Verdana and Arial. The door-hanger wordplay beyond the two headlines and the chips. Gradients, shadows, decorative imagery in the data area. Bar, alcohol or political imagery (contest rules).

## 5 · How you worked

Draft, max 5 regels:
1. Claude Code (Fable 5.1) with the brief PDF, the CSV and the visual guidelines; it computed CPI, IPM and implied first rides per creative and flagged NG-15 as impossible.
2. Loudbox MCP (`loudbox-demo`): `creativeLibrary.detail` and `get_creative_preview` to check what the 20 creatives actually show, `voiceOfCustomer.dashboard` for inDrive and Bolt review themes, `brandAssets.list` for the modular kit.
3. Hypotheses and budget drafted by Claude, verified by me against the brief's brand-fit rules and the message split.
4. Brief tested by handing it blind to Claude Design and logging every assumption it had to make, then closing those gaps in the text.
5. One thing the AI got wrong: Claude proposed a Claude Design prompt for a design system, three ad sizes and a storyboard; the brief asks for a written one-format brief and says "no slides, no deck". I caught it and the prompt was rewritten as an executability test of the brief text.

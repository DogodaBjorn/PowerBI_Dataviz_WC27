# Creative brief for an AI creator, v3 (self-contained)

Nederlandse toelichting: deze versie staat op zichzelf. Een AI-creator die alleen deze negen velden krijgt, zonder de hypotheses, de dataset of de brand kit, kan de ad maken. Verwijzingen naar NG-codes en hypothesenummers zijn vervangen door de reden in gewone woorden; merkspecificaties, alle tekststrings en de oplevereisen staan in de velden zelf. Onderaan staat wat v3 anders doet dan v2.

---

**Which hypothesis this brief brings to life (one line)**
Hypothesis 1 of 3, "Your price. Your driver.": a static ad in which a young Lagos commuter sets his own fare on the inDrive app next to a fare he cannot touch, and three drivers respond to his offer.

**Hypothesis**
Hypothesis 1, chosen because it is the only idea from last quarter with proven results: a static showing the inDrive fare slider next to a fixed-price app was the cheapest creative of the quarter, and the one headline that named both "your price" and "your driver" had the best install rate. This brief rebuilds that idea with a person, a shared ride, drivers responding and the difference in the headline. Background for the creator: inDrive is a ride-hailing app where the passenger proposes the fare, nearby drivers accept or counter, and the passenger then picks the driver by price, rating, arrival time or car. Every inDrive ad must show one of those two things happening, clearly and large: the passenger setting the price, or the passenger choosing the driver. This ad shows both.

**Format and size**
Static image. Master: 9:16, 1080 x 1920 px. Derived cut: 1:1, 1080 x 1080 px. Two language versions of each size (English, Nigerian Pidgin), so four finals. Deliver each as PNG (sRGB, max 2 MB, no visible compression on text) plus the layered source file (PSD or Figma). File names: indrive-ng-yourprice-9x16-en.png, -9x16-pcm.png, -1x1-en.png, -1x1-pcm.png.

**Channel**
Meta (Instagram Stories and Reels, Facebook Stories: 9:16; Facebook and Instagram feed: 1:1) and Google App campaigns in Nigeria (portrait 9:16 and square 1:1). Audience: new passengers in Lagos and Abuja, 20 to 27, English and Pidgin speakers.

**Who is in it**
Two Nigerian men, both 23 to 25, on a Lagos pavement at dusk. Chidi, the main character, Igbo appearance, junior office worker: clean short-sleeve shirt, dark chinos, white sneakers, small backpack on one shoulder. He holds a phone in his right hand at chest height, right thumb on the screen, eyes on the phone, half smile. His friend, Yoruba appearance, same age, polo shirt and jeans, stands at his left shoulder looking at the same screen. Setting: a busy roadside in the style of Ikeja or Yaba: shop fronts with blurred, unreadable signage, warm street light, traffic as soft bokeh in the background, no yellow buses or taxis in focus. Both stand on the pavement; nobody is inside a car. Photographic realism, natural skin tones, no plastic AI sheen, correct hands and fingers. Faces must read as Nigerian; no light-skinned or non-African features. The drivers named on screen are fictional: Musa (Hausa name), Tunde (Yoruba), Emeka (Igbo).

**The inDrive moment**
The inDrive fare screen is the largest single element in the image, at least 45% of the canvas, placed lower-left and overlapping Chidi's hand so his thumb visibly rests on it. It must be readable when the image is scaled to 200 px wide. Build it as a white rounded card (corner radius about 32 px at 1080 width) with, from top to bottom: small grey label "Your offer"; the amount "₦4,100" in bold black type, the biggest text on the page after the headline; a horizontal slider track in light green (#E4FF88) with a dark knob two thirds to the right, a round "–" button at the left end and a round "+" button at the right end; a full-width pill button in Drive green (#C1F11D) with black text "Agree on price"; below it three stacked driver rows, each a thin white row with a round avatar placeholder (initial letter only, no photo), name, rating with a star, arrival time, and a small green "Accept" pill: "Musa  4.9★  3 min  Accept", "Tunde  4.8★  5 min  Accept", "Emeka  4.7★  6 min  Accept". Chidi's thumb rests on the first "Accept". Directly above the inDrive card, a narrow grey band (#B3B8BF background, about 12% of canvas height, no brand, no logo, no colour accent) represents an unnamed other app: text in dark grey "Fare ₦5,600 · price updated ↑", a small lock icon, and a greyed-out button. No car, no taxi symbol, no name on this band. The viewer sees, in one glance, a price nobody can change next to a price Chidi is setting with his own thumb, and three drivers already saying yes to it.

**Scene by scene (static: headline · visual · call to action)**
Canvas: solid Drive green #C1F11D. Wordmark: the word "inDrive" in black, Poppins Bold (PP Agrandir Bold if available), lower-case i, capital D, top-left, large (about 40% of canvas width on 9:16, 35% on 1:1), touching the left edge of the canvas, nothing overlapping it. Headline, directly under the wordmark, Poppins Bold, black, two lines maximum, about 96 px on 9:16: English version "Your price. Your driver." / Pidgin version "Na your price, na your driver." Subhead under the headline, Poppins SemiBold, black, about 44 px: English "Offer your price. Drivers respond. You choose." / Pidgin "Offer your price. Drivers go answer. Na you choose." People: Chidi and his friend right of centre, framed from the waist up, occupying the right 55% of the canvas and the vertical middle; the fare card overlaps the lower-left of the pair so that Chidi's hand and the card form one unit; the grey band sits above the card at the left. Green background, people and card must read as one scene, not three boxes: add a soft shadow under the card and let Chidi's hand cross the card edge. Call to action: black pill button, Poppins SemiBold, white text "Set your price on inDrive", about 40 px text, bottom-left inside the safe area. Label: bottom-right, Poppins Regular, about 24 px, black at 60% opacity: "Created using AI". Safe areas: on 9:16 keep all text out of the top 14% and bottom 20% of the canvas and 15% from the right edge (platform interface sits there); on 1:1 keep an 8% margin on all sides. Colours allowed: Drive green #C1F11D, Light green #E4FF88, Light warm beige #FFFEE9 (optional card tint), Clouded grey #B3B8BF (other-app band), black #000000, white #FFFFFF. No other hues. 1:1 cut: same elements; wordmark top-left, headline under it, people on the right, fare card lower-left at 40% of the canvas, the grey band reduced to a single line above the card; drop the subhead if it collides with the card, never the headline or the amount. All amounts in naira with the ₦ sign, between ₦3,200 and ₦4,600; no other currency.

**Music and voice**
None: this is a static image. If a 6-second motion cut is requested later: original Afrobeats-style instrumental, no recognisable artist, no lyrics, no Amapiano or any South African music; no voice-over; headline language follows the version.

**Must not appear**
Words anywhere in the image, in any language: "taxi", "commission", "negotiate" (not even as a quote), "cheap", "cheap rides", "discount", "save", "faster", "verified drivers", "SOS", "share your trip", "safe", "safety". Any competitor name, logo, colour scheme or recognisable app layout; any direct comparison in words; a taxi-checker pattern, yellow taxi or car on the other-app band. A headline about ease, comfort, speed or safety instead of the price and driver lines above. Several prices on screen without the slider and the "Your offer" label. The fare card smaller than 45% of the canvas, or the amount unreadable at 200 px wide. Cars that look luxury or newer than 2021, car badges, licence plates. Real street names, shop names, landmarks or addresses, in the photo or on any map. Driver details beyond first name, rating and arrival time: no surname, phone number, plate, or photo of a real person; no placeholder names such as "Mateo" or "Kate". Non-Nigerian faces, visibly synthetic faces, extra fingers, warped hands, text rendered by the image model (all text is set in the layout, not generated in the picture). A phone with a visible manufacturer logo, distinctive notch or camera layout: render a generic device. Dollar signs or any currency other than naira. Churches, mosques, religious buildings, alcohol, smoking, profanity. Anyone inside a car without a seat belt, driver included. A footnote or small print as the only place where price-setting or driver choice appears. Text outside the safe areas. Any other colour than the six listed. Before delivery, list every text string in the finals and confirm none of the banned words appears, confirm the fare card is the largest element, and confirm the wordmark is uncropped and not overlapped.

## Wat v3 anders doet dan v2

1. **Geen afhankelijkheden meer.** NG-codes, "Insight 1/4" en "brand kit" zijn weg; de reden staat in gewone woorden en de merkspecificatie staat in het veld zelf.
2. **Uitleg van inDrive in het veld Hypothesis**, twee zinnen, zodat een creator die het merk niet kent weet waarom het scherm groot moet.
3. **De fare-kaart is opgebouwd als componentbeschrijving:** kaartradius, kleuren per onderdeel, volgorde, avatar als initiaal (geen foto), exacte rijteksten.
4. **Typografie met maten** (wordmark 40% breedte, headline 96 px, subhead 44 px, CTA 40 px, label 24 px) en een kleurenlijst van zes toegestane kleuren met hex.
5. **Bestandsnamen en bestandseisen** (PNG sRGB max 2 MB, gelaagd bestand).
6. **Doelgroepregel in Channel**, want de creator moet weten voor wie hij rendert.
7. **Extra verboden** die bij AI-beeld standaard misgaan: tekst door het beeldmodel laten renderen, lichte huid, "safe/safety" als woord.
8. **Een zelfcontrole aan het eind van Must not appear**, zodat de check onderdeel van de brief is en niet van een apart gesprek.

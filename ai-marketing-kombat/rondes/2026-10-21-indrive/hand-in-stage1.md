**Ranking.** 1 first: it is the only recipe with proof (NG-01, NG-02, NG-07), the petrol and Bolt updates make "a price you both agree on" the message of the moment, and it can run on two channels at once. 2 second: the largest untested opportunity (Comfort, Ngozi, the Uber gap) on the largest Q4 channel. 3 third: strong hook evidence, but it depends on execution in the first two seconds and only runs on one channel.

**Why three different ideas:** different persona or USP, different channel and format, different Q4 moment (daily commute, festive family gatherings, late-evening rides home).

## 3. Creative brief for an AI creator

*Brings hypothesis 1, "Your price. Your driver.", to life: the split-screen fare concept that won Q3 (NG-01 static, NG-02 9:16), now with people, a situation, drivers responding and the USP in the headline.*

| Field | Brief |
|---|---|
| **Hypothesis** | Hypothesis 1. Chidi feels ride apps decide the price for him. Showing the price being set by him, next to a price nobody can touch, is the only Q3 idea that beat target twice (NG-01 $0.97 static, NG-02 $1.70 in 9:16), and the only headline that names both USPs (NG-07, 9:16) had the best IPM of the quarter. The 9:16 master follows NG-02 and NG-07; the 1:1 cut carries the recipe to Google. |
| **Format and size** | Static, 9:16 (1080x1920) master; a 1:1 (1080x1080) cut derived from it for Google App campaigns and Meta feed. Two language variants of every size: English and Pidgin. Deliver each variant as PNG and as a layered file. |
| **Channel** | Meta stories and feed (9:16 and 1:1) and Google App campaigns (1:1). |
| **Who is in it** | Chidi, 24, Nigerian man (cast Yoruba or Igbo; the driver on the card from another group, e.g. Hausa name "Musa", or "Tunde" if Chidi is Igbo), with a friend of the same age next to him (most rides are shared). Smart-casual: clean shirt, sneakers, small backpack. Phone in hand, looking at the screen with a half smile. Setting: a busy Lagos roadside at dusk (Ikeja or Yaba type street: shop fronts, yellow street light, traffic blurred in the background). No real street names, shop logos, licence plates. No alcohol, no smoking, no church or mosque in frame. |
| **The inDrive moment** | The lower two thirds of the canvas is the inDrive fare screen, filling at least 45% of the total image and readable at thumbnail size. It shows: "Your offer" with the amount **₦4,100** in the largest type after the headline, a slider with "–" and "+" ends, the button "Agree on price", and under it three driver responses stacked: "Tunde · 4.9 ★ · 3 min · Accept", "Musa · 4.8 ★ · 5 min · Accept", "Emeka · 4.7 ★ · 6 min · Accept"; Chidi's thumb rests on the top one. Above it, a narrow band is the anonymous grey app: "Fare ₦5,600 ↑ price updated", a lock icon, greyed-out button, no brand, no taxi checker pattern, no car. The viewer sees the slider under Chidi's thumb and drivers responding to it: the price is being set by him, and the driver is being chosen by him, right now. |
| **Headline, visual, call to action** | Headline (PP Agrandir or Poppins Bold, top of the image): EN "Your price. Your driver." / Pidgin "Na your price, na your driver." Subhead (Poppins SemiBold): EN "Offer your price. Drivers respond. You choose." / Pidgin "Offer your price. Drivers go answer. Na you choose." Visual: split as described, inDrive green (brand kit) on the fare screen, neutral grey on the "other app" band, Chidi and his friend standing across the split so the two parts read as one scene. CTA button (Poppins SemiBold): "Set your price on inDrive". Logo: black wordmark top left against the edge on green; white only if placed on the photo. Bottom right, small, Poppins Regular: "Created using AI". All amounts in naira (₦), between ₦3,200 and ₦4,600. |
| **Music and voice** | None (static). If a 6 s motion cut is made: an original Afrobeats-style instrumental, no recognisable artist, no lyrics, no Amapiano or other South African music; headline language as per variant. |
| **Must not appear** | The words "taxi", "commission", "negotiate" (not even as a quote), "cheap rides", "discount", "save", "faster"; any competitor name, logo, colour or app layout; a taxi checker pattern or yellow taxi on the "other app" side; a headline about ease, comfort, speed or safety above the price moment; several prices on screen without the slider (that does not count as setting the price); the app screen smaller than 45% of the canvas or the amount unreadable; luxury or 2022+ cars, car badges, licence plates, real addresses, driver surnames or phone numbers; non-Nigerian faces, placeholder names from the kit ("Mateo"); dollar signs; churches, mosques, alcohol, smoking; a phone with a visible manufacturer logo or notch that identifies the brand (render a generic phone); real street names or landmarks in the map mock; driver details beyond first name, rating and arrival time (no surname, photo of a real person, plate or phone number); anyone in a car without a seat belt, driver included; an end-card footnote as the only brand-fit element. |

## 4. Updates

- **Uber shut down in Nigeria.** Changed: hypothesis 2 now targets the freed-up comfort riders in Abuja and Lagos with "Comfort at a price you set", because their quotes show they do not yet know they set the price and choose the driver; no competitor is named.
- **Bolt: 40% off the first three rides** (Meta Ad Library shows it is capped at ₦3,000). Changed nothing in the budget; sharpened hypothesis 1's headline to "Your price. Your driver." because a discount alone is a brand misfit and price control works on every ride, not three. Expect Meta CPMs to rise and keep Meta spend tests short.
- **Petrol up to ₦1,350 per litre.** Changed the hypothesis 1 visual: the "other app" side now shows a fare that just went up, because jumping prices are Chidi's pain point; copy says "a price you both agree on", never "cheap", since drivers' costs rose too.

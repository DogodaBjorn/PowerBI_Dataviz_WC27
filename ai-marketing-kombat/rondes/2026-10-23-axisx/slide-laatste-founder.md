# Last slide: to the founder

## Title
The one line we can't write for you

## Body
Every opening in this plan starts with a real woman. The first one should be you.

What your brief told us: a month where everything changes. Notes in five places. "I'm fine", said for too long. Two weeks of logging that finally make sense to a doctor.

Your sentence opens the founder video, the first message to every community lead and the Founding 500 page. It is about you, not about the app.

**Three questions to find it**
1. What moment made you start building AxisX?
2. What did you need in your hand then, and didn't have?
3. What do you wish someone had said to you, or to her, at that moment?

**Your line**
- About you, not AxisX
- Under 3 seconds out loud
- Something only you could say

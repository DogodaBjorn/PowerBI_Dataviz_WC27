# Influencer and Creator Marketing (Spark Ads, Partnership Ads, whitelisting)

Status: reference for 2-hour case rounds. Written 2026-10-23. [CHECK] = verify before relying on it. The case's own numbers beat everything below.

## 1. What it is good for
- Funnel role: trust, social proof and creative supply. Upper-mid funnel by default; lower funnel when paired with paid amplification, discount codes and links.
- Intent: low to medium; strong for consideration (demos, reviews, "get ready with me", tutorials, unboxings).
- Audience: defined by the creator's community; niche and local audiences reachable; strong with Gen Z/millennials; strong in emerging markets (Nigeria: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, WhatsApp communities) [CHECK].
- Best for: app launches, beauty/fashion, food/delivery, gaming, fintech consumer apps, DTC, local brands, new market entry where trust is low (ride-hailing safety and pricing messaging via local creators).
- Weak for: precise attribution, scale predictability, compliance-heavy categories (finance, health) without legal review; B2B except thought leaders.

## 2. Campaign structure & objectives
- Tiers (follower count, rule of thumb): nano (<10k), micro (10k-100k), macro (100k-1M), mega/celebrity (>1M). Nano/micro: highest engagement per follower and cheapest per post; macro/mega: reach and credibility.
- Deal structures: flat fee per deliverable, gifting/product seeding, affiliate/commission (CPA/revenue share), hybrid (fee + commission), ambassador (long-term), performance retainer, usage rights licence (paid media), exclusivity.
- Paid-amplified creator content ("whitelisting"/"creator licensing"): run ads from creator's handle:
  - Meta: Partnership ads (formerly Branded Content ads; Instagram Collab and brand-partner permissions), creator-handle ads with identity authorisation [CHECK exact 2026 naming].
  - TikTok: Spark Ads (authorisation code 7/30/60/365 days); TikTok One / Creator Marketplace (TCM); Branded Content toggle; Spark Ads with Shop links.
  - YouTube: Partnership Ads in Google Ads / Demand Gen using creator videos; BrandConnect [CHECK].
  - Snapchat: Partnership ads / Creator marketplace [CHECK]; Pinterest: Partnership ads with creators [CHECK]; X/Reddit: limited.
- Structure that works: seed 10-30 micro-creators for creative diversity > identify top performers by engagement/hook rate > license and whitelist top 3-5 > scale as paid creative > re-brief with learnings.
- Briefing: objective, key message (1-2 points), mandatories (disclosure, tags, claims), freedom elements (hook, language, humour), deliverables, usage rights, timeline, approval process.

## 3. Bidding & budget
- Organic creator posts have no bidding; paid amplification follows platform bidding (Meta/TikTok rules above).
- Budget split rule of thumb: creator fees 30-60% vs paid amplification 40-70% in performance programmes [rule of thumb, no source]. Creator content should be treated as creative production cost plus distribution.
- Cost models: CPM-equivalent (fee / views), CPE, CPV, CPC (link), CPA/commission. Compare creators on cost per outcome, not follower count.
- Test budget: 5-10 creators x small fee; scale after validation.
- Usage rights: paid usage typically adds 20-100%+ on top of base fee depending on duration and platforms; exclusivity adds fees [CHECK; negotiate].
- Time: allow 2-4 weeks for outreach, briefing, content production and approval; in a 2-hour round, assume simulated timelines and show a plan.

## 4. Targeting & signals
- Creator selection: audience demographics (country, age, gender), engagement rate, view-to-follower ratio (median views / followers), audience quality (fake follower check), brand fit, content quality, past performance, brand safety history, local language.
- Tools: TikTok Creator Marketplace, Meta Creator Marketplace, YouTube BrandConnect, Instagram Collab, third-party platforms (Upfluence, CreatorIQ, Grin, Aspire, Modash), local agencies [CHECK].
- Paid targeting for whitelisted ads: use platform broad/lookalike/custom audiences; creator's lookalike audience as a seed when permitted.
- Tracking: unique links with UTMs, promo/affiliate codes, deep links/MMP links for apps, landing pages per creator, post-purchase "How did you hear about us?" survey.

## 5. Creative: what wins
- Creator-led, first-person, authentic voice in local language; product used in context; honest pros/cons; real reactions.
- Hook in first 1-3 seconds: problem, surprising claim, result, "POV", question. Keep brand in the first 5 seconds for platforms where branding matters.
- Formats: short vertical video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) 15-45 s; carousels; stories with link stickers; long-form YouTube integrations (60-90 s mid-roll or dedicated); live shopping/ lives.
- Give creators the message and key proof points, not a script; allow their style. Provide 3-5 hook options.
- Request raw files and alternate cuts (clean cut without text, 9:16 and 4:5) to repurpose in ads.
- Diversify creators by persona (age, gender, geography, use case) to find message-market fit (Andromeda/creative-as-targeting logic).
- Fatigue: creator content fatigues more slowly than brand creative but still decays after 2-4 weeks in paid; rotate creators.

## 7. Measurement & attribution
- Organic metrics: views, reach, view-through rate, engagement rate, saves, shares, comments sentiment, link clicks.
- Direct response: UTMs, codes, deep links, affiliate tracking; platform attribution for whitelisted ads.
- Incrementality: geo tests (markets with/without creators), time-based pre/post, holdout (code-based), survey lift, branded search lift and direct traffic changes.
- Dark social: sharing in WhatsApp/DM is not tracked; use surveys and code redemptions.
- Whitelisted ad performance usually beats brand-handle ads due to social proof; verify with an A/B test (same creative, brand handle vs creator handle) [do not assume uplift size].

## 8. DO's
1. Choose creators by audience match and demonstrated performance (median views, not follower count).
2. Contract usage rights, exclusivity window, approval process, deliverables, disclosure, payment terms.
3. Secure Spark/Partnership authorisation early; test creator content as paid before scaling.
4. Seed many micro-creators for creative diversity, then scale winners.
5. Provide a short brief: 1-2 key messages, mandatories, freedom.
6. Track with codes/links/deep links/surveys.
7. Disclose clearly per local rules.
8. Localise (language, humour, context); use local creators for local trust.
9. Repurpose creator content across Meta, TikTok, YouTube, with permission.
10. Run creator content continuously, not as a one-off burst.

## 9. DON'Ts
1. Do not pick creators by follower count alone.
2. Do not over-script; killing authenticity kills performance.
3. Do not skip disclosure or use misleading claims (legal liability for both brand and creator).
4. Do not buy followers' vanity metrics (check fake followers).
5. Do not treat creator spend as only awareness without tracking.
6. Do not whitelist without contract on usage and duration.
7. Do not put all budget on one mega-creator.
8. Do not wait until week 3 to test amplification.
9. Do not use unlicensed music in paid usage.
10. Do not compare creator CPM with media CPM; creator cost buys creative plus trust.

## 10. Method of success (playbook)
1. Define goal, KPI and target persona; choose platforms.
2. Shortlist 20-30 creators; vet audience, engagement, safety; contract 8-15 for seeding.
3. Brief and produce; secure rights and codes (Spark/Partnership).
4. Launch organic + paid (whitelisted) with small budgets; compare hook rate, CTR, CPA vs brand ads.
5. Scale winners: license, raise budgets, create variations with the same creators.
6. Refresh: new creators and angles every 2-4 weeks; reuse best hooks.
7. Validate: codes, surveys, geo/time tests.

## 11. KPI benchmarks (orders of magnitude; prefer the case's own benchmarks)
- Creator fee ranges vary hugely by market and platform: nano from free/product to ~$50-$300 per post; micro ~$200-$2,000; macro ~$2,000-$20,000+; mega beyond; emerging markets generally lower [CHECK; negotiate].
- Engagement rate: Instagram ~1-4%, TikTok ~3-8% (by views/followers), YouTube ~1-4% [rule of thumb].
- Whitelisted creator ads often show higher CTR and lower CPA than brand-handle ads in tests, but magnitude varies; always test.
- Cost per view via creators: ~$0.01-$0.05 organic equivalent; vs paid CPV [rough].

## 12. AI features
- AI creator matching and discovery (TikTok One, Meta Creator Marketplace, third-party tools), AI-generated creator-like content (AI avatars via TikTok Symphony, virtual influencers), AI script/hook generators, AI dubbing to localise creator videos.
- Disclosure: platforms require labelling of realistic AI-generated or AI-altered content; brand partnership labels still required. EU AI Act and local regulators (ASA UK, FTC US, Reclamecode NL, ARCON Nigeria [CHECK]) expect disclosure of paid partnerships and transparency about AI-generated influencers.
- Practical: authentic human creators outperform generic AI avatars for trust-driven categories; test.

## 13. Common traps in a case/competition setting
- Choosing macro/mega by reach without cost-per-outcome logic.
- No measurement plan (codes, links, deep links) so impact is unprovable.
- Treating creator content as only organic and not planning paid amplification.
- Ignoring disclosure and rights (usage, exclusivity) in the plan.
- Counting impressions and views as success for a CPA-judged case.
- Failing to propose a creative testing structure (multiple creators, hooks).

# Pinterest Ads

Status: reference for 2-hour case rounds. Written 2026-10-23. [CHECK] = verify before relying on it. The case's own numbers beat everything below.

## 1. What it is good for
- Funnel role: planning and discovery; upper-mid funnel with strong shopping intent. Users save ideas 1-3 months before purchases (home, wedding, fashion, food, travel, beauty, DIY).
- Intent: medium, forward-looking ("what should I buy/do?"). Search-like behaviour via keywords; long content shelf life (Pins keep delivering for weeks/months).
- Audience: skews female (~60-70%), 25-54, higher household income; strongest in US, Western Europe, LatAm (Brazil, Mexico), growing elsewhere. Weak penetration in some emerging markets (check availability for the case market).
- Best for: retail/e-commerce with visual products, home/decor, fashion, beauty, food/recipes, travel, weddings, parenting, seasonal campaigns, DIY.
- Weak for: B2B, app installs in young male-heavy markets, utility/ride apps, urgent-intent categories.

## 2. Campaign structure & objectives (2025-2026)
- Objectives: Awareness (Brand awareness), Consideration (Video views, Traffic, Engagement) [CHECK], Conversions (Web conversions, Catalog sales). Performance+ (automated campaigns) launched 2025 [CHECK name/availability].
- Formats: Standard image Pins, Video Pins, Max Width video, Carousel, Collections, Idea Ads, Shopping Ads (catalog-driven), Premiere Spotlight/Showcase (reservation), Quiz ads (trial) [CHECK].
- Performance+ campaigns: automate audience, placements and bidding at campaign level with creative and conversion goal; reduces manual targeting [CHECK].
- Structure: 1 campaign per goal (Catalog sales / conversions / traffic), 2-4 ad groups (targeting variants), 4-8 pins per group; keep feed healthy for shopping.
- Seasonality: start planning and ramping budget 45-90 days before peak events (Pinterest users plan early).
- Catalog: product feed (via Merchant/feeds) with titles, images, availability; Product groups for bids.

## 3. Bidding & budget
- Bidding: Automatic bid (lowest cost), Maximum bid / Target CPA (Conversions), Manual bid [CHECK naming]. Goal-based: cost per click, CPM, CPV; conversion optimisation on website events (checkout).
- Learning: roughly 50 conversions per week per ad group helps stabilise; start with add-to-cart event if checkout volume is low.
- Budget: daily budget at least ~5-10x target CPA; minimum daily budget about $2 per ad group [CHECK].
- Scale slowly: 20% increases every few days.
- Longer horizons: expect delayed conversions; keep campaigns running for 3-4 weeks for evaluation.

## 4. Targeting & signals
- Targeting options: keywords (high intent; search and related pins), interests (categories), demographics (age, gender, location, device), custom audiences (customer list, site visitors, engaged users), Actalike audiences (lookalike), placement (browse, search, related Pins).
- Signals: Pinterest Tag + Conversions API (CAPI); catalog feed events; offline conversions [CHECK].
- Broad + keywords combination works; "Expanded targeting" lets Pinterest widen reach.
- Exclusions: customers, low-intent audiences; negative keywords.

## 5. Creative: what wins
- Vertical 2:3 (1000x1500) image or 9:16 video; clean, aspirational, high-quality imagery; lifestyle shots over plain product shots.
- Text overlay with a clear benefit/idea; avoid clutter; brand logo subtle.
- Titles and descriptions are indexed for search: use keyword-rich, natural phrasing; first ~40 chars of the title and first ~50-60 chars of the description are visible.
- Video: 6-15 s, vertical, no heavy intro; sound optional (many watch muted); show process/outcome ("how to", "ideas").
- Collection/Shopping: multiple products, consistent styling.
- Creator/Idea pins: how-to sequences work; collaborate with creators for authenticity (Pinterest Creator Marketplace, partnership ads [CHECK]).
- Refresh: pins can run longer than on other platforms but test new visuals every 3-4 weeks.
- Avoid: fake before/after, clickbait, overlay text covering the product, misleading UI.

## 6. Ad specs cheat sheet
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Image | 2:3 (1000x1500); also 1:1 and 9:16; JPG/PNG up to 20 MB |
| Video | 9:16 (1080x1920) or 1:1, 4:5; 4 s to 15 min; recommended 6-15 s; MP4/MOV up to 2 GB |

## 7. Measurement & attribution
- Default windows: 30-day click, 30-day engagement, 1-day view [CHECK]. Configurable; the attribution is multi-touch and engagement-based (saves, closeups).
- Tools: Pinterest Conversion Lift (user-level holdouts), Brand Lift studies, Pinterest MMM partnerships [CHECK].
- Typical effect: long consideration; heavy assisted conversions; compare against search/direct lift.
- Check platform metrics: outbound click CPC, saves, closeups, engagement rate; track add-to-cart and purchase with Tag + CAPI.

## 8. DO's
1. Plan seasonal campaigns 45-90 days ahead.
2. Use keyword + interest targeting with Performance+/broad expansion.
3. Optimise Pin titles/descriptions for search keywords.
4. Use vertical high-quality creative and lifestyle imagery.
5. Maintain a clean, updated product feed; check diagnostics.
6. Install Tag + CAPI; verify events and deduplication.
7. Use Shopping/Collections for catalog goals.
8. Run campaigns 3-4 weeks before evaluating; allow delayed conversions.
9. Test creator content via partnership/whitelisted ads.
10. Exclude existing customers from prospecting.

## 9. DON'Ts
1. Do not use horizontal creative for feed.
2. Do not judge with 7-day windows only; Pinterest influence is slower.
3. Do not neglect search keywords; they are the intent signal.
4. Do not set tiny budgets across many ad groups.
5. Do not use generic stock photos or overly salesy banners.
6. Do not expect urgent-intent performance; this is a planning channel.
7. Do not forget to verify Pinterest availability and reach in the case market.
8. Do not ignore feed disapprovals (shopping ads fail silently).

## 10. Method of success (playbook)
1. Launch: Tag + CAPI, catalog sync, 6-8 vertical pins per theme; keyword + interest combined targeting; automated bidding.
2. Learn (7-14 days): monitor CTR, CPC, saves, add-to-cart; adjust keywords.
3. Optimise: cut weak pins; add winners as Idea/Collection formats; add negative keywords.
4. Scale: increase budget 20% steps; add seasonal themes and new categories; test Performance+.
5. Refresh: new pins every 3-4 weeks.
6. Validate: conversion lift or compare assisted conversions.

## 11. KPI benchmarks (orders of magnitude; prefer the case's own benchmarks)
- CPM: about $2-$10 (US/EU higher); CPC ~$0.1-$1.5; CTR ~0.2-1%; shopping ads CVR comparable to social.
- Costs are lower than Meta in many categories but scale is limited by audience.
- Strong view-to-save and long tail; ROAS often improves over weeks.

## 12. AI features on this platform
- Pinterest Performance+ (AI-driven targeting/bidding/creative), Pinterest Assistant / Pinterest Lens (visual search), AI-generated image/background tools for advertisers [CHECK], automated product tagging/auto-generated descriptions.
- AI labelling: Pinterest labels AI-modified/generated Pins ("AI modified") and offers user controls to reduce AI content; ads policy expects disclosure for AI-generated imagery where misleading [CHECK].

## 13. Common traps in a case/competition setting
- Choosing Pinterest for an app/ride KPI in a market where Pinterest has low reach.
- Judging on last-click in a short window (planning channel, slow conversion).
- Ignoring catalog quality.
- Over-weighting saves/engagement vs conversions.
- Underestimating lead time (campaign needs weeks).

# Snapchat Ads

Status: reference for 2-hour case rounds. Written 2026-10-23. [CHECK] = verify before relying on it. The case's own numbers beat everything below.

## 1. What it is good for
- Funnel role: young-audience reach, awareness, AR engagement, app installs, and lower funnel via goal-based bidding.
- Intent: low (social/messaging context), high attention on full-screen vertical, camera-first behaviour.
- Audience: skews 13-34 (Gen Z and younger millennials); strong in US, UK, France, Saudi Arabia/Gulf, India, Pakistan, parts of Africa (check market) [CHECK].
- Best for: Gen Z brands, entertainment, gaming, consumer apps, fashion/beauty, quick-service food, events, AR try-on and Lenses, Middle East campaigns (KSA, UAE).
- Weak for: 35+ audiences, B2B, high-consideration products, markets with low Snap penetration (verify Nigeria and other case markets).

## 2. Campaign structure & objectives (2025-2026)
- Objectives (Ads Manager): Awareness & Engagement (Reach, Video views, Engagement), Traffic, App promotion (App installs, App purchases), Leads, Sales (Promote catalog / Website conversions), Promote place.
- Formats: Snap Ads (single image/video, vertical), Story Ads, Collection Ads, Dynamic Product Ads (catalog), Spotlight Ads, Sponsored AR Lenses / Filters / Sponsored Snaps [CHECK], Commercials (non-skippable 6 s), Lead-gen Ads, Sponsored Snaps (chat).
- Advantage+ style automation: Snapchat Smart Campaign / Automated placements, Advantage+ campaign (AI-driven) [CHECK].
- Structure: 1 campaign per objective; 1-3 ad squads (ad sets); 4-6 creatives per squad; use Automatic placement unless a reason exists.
- Reach: audience network (third-party apps) can be turned on/off.

## 3. Bidding & budget
- Goal-based bidding: Auto-bid, Max bid (bid cap), Target cost (cost cap), Min ROAS (for sales).
- Learning: ad squad needs about 50 optimisation events per week; first 24-72 h learning, avoid edits [CHECK].
- Budget: minimum daily budget about $5 per ad squad [CHECK]; for conversion optimisation plan budget >= ~7x target CPA per day.
- Scale gradually (20-30% increments); expand audience and placements before cost-capping aggressively.
- Optimise to the deepest event with volume; consider "swipe up"/click events for traffic, "app install" before "purchase" if volume is low.

## 4. Targeting & signals
- Demographics: age 13+ (18+ for many categories), gender, language, location, OS/device; Snap Lifestyle Categories (interest/behaviour), Snap Audience Match (customer lists), Lookalike audiences, Pixel/SDK audiences, engagement audiences.
- Broad + auto placement works with sufficient creative; Lookalikes useful for scale.
- Signals: Snap Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI); App: Snap SDK or MMP (AppsFlyer, Adjust, etc.); iOS SKAN support.
- Exclusions: current customers, installers.

## 5. Creative: what wins
- Vertical 9:16 full-screen video; first 2 seconds; sound on (Snap is sound-on); native/Snap-style (camera shot, selfie-style).
- Length: 5-10 s ideal; up to 180 s allowed; shorter wins on cost. Fast cuts, bold text, creator POV, humour.
- Show product immediately; highlight offer/price and CTA in-frame; use Snap-native features (Lenses, AR try-on, shoppable AR).
- Safe zones: keep important content away from top ~150 px and bottom ~250 px [CHECK] (UI elements). Snap provides safe zone guidance.
- Creator/UGC: authentic reaction content; creators via Snap Creator Marketplace/Partnership ads [CHECK].
- Refresh every 1-2 weeks; track swipe-up rate, screen time, 2-sec view rate.
- Local language and culture; Arabic RTL support for MENA.

## 7. Measurement & attribution
- Default attribution: 28-day swipe up (click), 1-day view [CHECK]; other windows: 7-day/1-day and shorter. Compare with other platforms using the same window.
- Tools: Snap Pixel + CAPI, MMP for app, Snap Conversion Lift, Brand Lift studies, Snap Measurement Partners (MMPs), Snapchat Offline conversions.
- Platform-reported conversions include view-through: separate click only.
- Best practice: use lift or geo-tests for incrementality; blended CAC comparison.

## 8. DO's
1. Build snap-native vertical video with sound and fast hooks.
2. Optimise for the deepest event with volume; use Auto-bid at launch.
3. Use Pixel + CAPI (or MMP) with validated events.
4. Test Lenses/AR for engagement-driven brands.
5. Use Lookalikes and Lifestyle categories; combine with broad auto placement.
6. Refresh creatives every 1-2 weeks.
7. Localise for language and region (Arabic, French, etc.).
8. Verify audience reach in the target market before allocating budget.
9. Exclude installers/customers for prospecting.

## 9. DON'Ts
1. Do not reuse horizontal or heavily branded TV ads.
2. Do not overuse Audience Network without quality checks.
3. Do not set tight caps on a new squad (starves delivery).
4. Do not judge by swipe rate or installs only.
5. Do not ignore Snap penetration in the market.
6. Do not run too many squads with low budget.

## 10. Method of success (playbook)
1. Launch with Auto-bid on 1-2 squads, 4-6 vertical creatives, Pixel/MMP validated.
2. Learn 3-7 days: hold edits; track install/purchase cost, CVR.
3. Optimise: kill weak creatives, test Target cost, add Lookalikes.
4. Scale: +20-30% budget steps; replicate winners; add Lenses/AR if brand-fit.
5. Refresh creatives weekly; validate through lift or backend CAC.

## 11. KPI benchmarks (orders of magnitude; prefer the case's own benchmarks)
- CPM about $2-$10 (higher in US/UK); swipe-up CTR ~0.3-1%; CPC ~$0.2-$1.2; CPI in MENA/India lower than US [CHECK].
- Conversion rates are lower than Google/Meta in many direct-response contexts; test with budget caution.
- Reach is cost-efficient among 13-34; frequency can saturate fast.

## 12. AI features on this platform
- Snap AI Lenses, generative AI Lenses (e.g. "Imagine Lens" for creators), AI-driven ad automation (Advantage+/Smart campaigns), Snap's generative creative tools for ads (image/video generation, text variants) [CHECK].
- AI labelling: Snap adds visual watermark/context cards on AI-generated images from its tools; ad policy against deceptive AI. [CHECK exact policy]

## 13. Common traps in a case/competition setting
- Over-allocating to Snapchat where its footprint in the case's market is small.
- Judging on swipe-up/installs only.
- Assuming it replicates Meta/TikTok performance.
- Misreading view-through attribution defaults.
- Using landscape/brand video.

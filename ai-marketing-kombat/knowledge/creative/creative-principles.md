# Performance creative principles (platform-agnostic) + luxury notes

Status: 2026-10-23, from background knowledge and platform best-practice guidance [CHECK specifics before quoting numbers].

## 1. Core principles

| Principle | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook in 0-2 s | First frame must show motion, intrigue or the product; no logo-only intros, no slow fades | Most viewers decide within 1-3 s on feed placements |
| USP in the headline | Say the distinctive benefit in plain words in the first line | Headline is often all that is read |
| Show the product/benefit moment long enough | Hold the hero shot and key benefit for at least 2-3 s so it can be read and understood | Fast cuts hide the proof; pendants and details need time to read |
| One message per asset | One idea, one proof, one CTA | Multi-message ads dilute recall |
| Sound-off design | Captions/on-screen text for the story, visuals carry meaning; sound adds | Large share of mobile feed views is sound-off [CHECK share per platform] |
| Native vs polished | Test both: native/UGC look often wins for performance in mass categories; luxury usually needs polished-but-human (film-like, intimate) rather than rough UGC | Platform behaviour vs brand control |
| Brand cues early | Logo/brand codes/colour/packaging visible in the first seconds and again at the end; avoid logo only at the end | YouTube ABCD research [CHECK source] |
| Platform-native formats | 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/TikTok/Stories, 1:1 or 4:5 for feed, 16:9 for YouTube/CTV; safe zones respected | Cropped content looks lazy |
| Clear CTA | One action with a clear next step | Especially for appointment funnels |

## 2. YouTube ABCD (Google framework)

- Attention: hook quickly, strong visuals, human faces, motion, surprising framing.
- Branding: brand and product introduced early and repeated naturally.
- Connection: emotion, story, relevance to the viewer.
- Direction: clear CTA or next step.
Use as a checklist for video; score each asset 0-2 on each letter before launch.

## 3. Creative diversification

- Matrix: concept x persona x format. Example for 3 concepts x 3 personas x 3 formats = 27 assets. Start with a thin slice (1 persona x 3 concepts x 2 formats) then expand on winners.
- Concepts: different big ideas (heritage, craft, gifting moment, collector's story), not just colour or copy tweaks. Platforms need real difference to deliver reach (similar creatives are clustered).
- Personas for luxury: the gifter (partner/family), the collector, the bride/groom, the self-purchaser, the Gulf client, the tourist buyer.
- Formats: video 6/15/30 s, static, carousel, story, collection, long-form hero film.

## 4. Creative fatigue

- Signs: frequency rising, CTR declining, CPA climbing, comments get negative.
- Premium audiences are small, so fatigue arrives quickly: refresh every 2-4 weeks for retargeting, 6-8 for prospecting [CHECK, set from data].
- Response: rotate concepts, pause fatigued asset, keep proven hooks, change first 2 seconds, add new proof.
- Frequency guard: cap per user per week (luxury 2-3 impressions/week typical for retargeting).

## 5. Testing ladder

1. Concept: which big idea resonates (heritage vs gifting vs craft).
2. Angle: which proof/benefit inside the winner (hours of handwork vs rarity vs story).
3. Hook: first 2 seconds variants.
4. Format/length: 6 vs 15 vs 30 s, 9:16 vs 4:5.
5. Iteration: copy, CTA, colour, music, pacing.
- Rules: change one variable per test; pre-register hypothesis, metric and decision threshold; use brand-lift or qualified-lead metrics, not only CTR; run until significance or minimum spend; write results to a creative log.
- Low volume: use fewer variants, longer tests, lead-quality scoring and incrementality (geo) tests.
- Metrics hierarchy for luxury: qualified leads and appointments > video completion/thumb-stop > CTR > CPC.

## 6. The executable creative brief (standalone for an AI creator)

A brief is "executable" when an AI creator (or freelancer) can produce the asset without further questions. Fields:

| Field | Content / example |
|---|---|
| ID + hypothesis | "Craft close-ups beat heritage story for qualified-lead rate among gifters (UK, 35-55)" |
| Objective + KPI | Qualified appointment; metric and target |
| Channel + placement | Instagram Reels, Stories; YouTube Shorts; Demand Gen |
| Format + size + duration | 9:16, 1080x1920, 8 s; safe zone top/bottom 250 px; sound-off |
| Audience/persona | Gifting husband, 40s, UK, affluent |
| Who is in it | Hands only / no faces / model description (age, locale) / none |
| The brand moment | What must be on screen, when, how long (e.g. logo 0-1 s, product hero 2-5 s) |
| Hook (0-2 s) | The exact opening visual |
| Headline | Exact words (e.g. "The Egg that started a legend") |
| Visual sequence | Shot list with seconds |
| Text on screen | Exact words, font, position (added in post) |
| CTA | "Book a private viewing" (soft); button label |
| Music + voice | Style, tempo, instrumentation, no lyrics; voice: none / whisper / male-female, language, accent |
| Reference assets | Real product photos (use as source, do not re-draw), mood board links, brand book |
| Style + colour | Palette, light, lens language |
| Must-not-appear | Competitor logos, brand logos on devices, text in scene, discount language, celebrities, political symbols, hands with extra fingers, stock clichés |
| Compliance | AI disclosure label, legal lines, licensed IP clearance |
| Review + approval | Who signs off; version number |
| Success criteria | Metric thresholds to scale or kill |

## 7. Luxury-specific creative notes

- Restraint: fewer elements, slower pacing than usual performance ads, but still a clear first-frame hook (a gem catching light, enamel being painted).
- Negative space: let the object breathe; one subject; dark or neutral backgrounds; typography minimal and in the brand font.
- Craftsmanship close-ups: macro on faceting, enamel, hands of the artisan, tools, sparks of the lathe, time shown (e.g. "200 hours").
- Show scale and wearing: on a neck/hand, with natural light; the moment of gifting (the box, the opening, the reaction).
- No hard-sell CTAs (avoid "Buy now", "Shop now", "Limited offer"); use "Discover", "Book a private viewing", "Speak with an advisor", "Explore the collection".
- No prices in prospecting; no discounts; no urgency timers.
- Sound: elegant silence or refined score; captions in restrained type.
- Authenticity: real jewellery photographed; AI only for context/backgrounds and clearly reviewed; no fake archive imagery.
- Heritage tone: factual, warm, no jingoism or politicised imagery; "House of Fabergé" voice (see faberge-primer.md sensitivities).
- Localisation: adapt imagery, model casting, calendar (Eid, Lunar New Year) and language with native review.
- Variant strategy under luxury constraints: keep one hero film per season; derive 6 s cut-downs and stills from it; test hooks and CTAs, not brand look.
- Measure creative on qualified-lead quality and brand-lift, not only CTR.

## 2. Positioning summary (working view)
- Mythic heritage (Romanov eggs) + modern coloured-gemstone jewellery + cinematic collaborations (GoT, 007) = "story-led high jewellery with an art-object soul".
- Ownership change in Aug 2025 = brand in transition: new owner investing, royal-client-first strategy, auction halo from the Winter Egg. Expect new creative direction and digital rebuild; jury may value a plan that fits a relaunch.
- Brand-specific levers: Easter (egg), Imperial heritage, craftsmanship (enamel, guilloché [bg]), coloured stones, bespoke commissions, collaborations.

## 4. Where AI can help: 14 candidate parts of Fabergé's marketing

Format: problem | AI application | KPI moved | risk / guardrail | 1-line example

### 4.1 Clienteling and CRM personalisation
- Problem: advisors have thin context; small client base buys rarely; outreach is generic.
- AI: client-360 summaries, next-best-conversation suggestions, drafting personal notes for advisor approval, propensity models by life event.
- KPI: repeat purchase rate, client lifetime value, appointment-to-sale rate, advisor response time.
- Guardrail: consent and GDPR, no creepy personalisation, advisor sends, AI drafts.
- Example: "Before a client's anniversary, the advisor receives a ready-to-edit message referencing a past emerald piece and a private viewing invite."

### 4.2 Gifting concierge / conversational commerce
- Problem: gifters are anxious (what, size, delivery by when), many abandon.
- AI: on-site and WhatsApp concierge that asks occasion/recipient/budget band, recommends 3 pieces, answers sizing and delivery, hands over to a human for high-value.
- KPI: gifting conversion, enquiry-to-appointment rate, AOV, CSAT.
- Guardrail: grounded in catalogue (RAG), no price improvisation, human handover, disclose AI.
- Example: "A husband types 'something for 25th anniversary, she likes green' and gets three emerald pieces with delivery-by-date and a 'speak to an advisor' button."

### 4.3 Product discovery / visual search
- Problem: pieces are unique, hard to describe; users search by look.
- AI: image-to-product search, "find similar" by style/stone/colour, semantic search ("art-nouveau enamel pendant").
- KPI: product page views per session, search-to-enquiry rate.
- Guardrail: only real catalogue; no hallucinated pieces.
- Example: "Upload a photo of a vintage egg charm and see the closest current pieces."

### 4.4 Provenance and craftsmanship storytelling
- Problem: the story (hours, hands, stones) is rich but unstructured and expensive to produce per piece.
- AI: ingest atelier notes, certificates and archive records; generate piece-level story pages, "digital passport" copy, multilingual versions; editors approve.
- KPI: time-on-page, enquiry rate on story pages, assisted conversions, brand-lift.
- Guardrail: facts must come from the provenance database; no invented lineage; legal sign-off.
- Example: "Each piece gets an auto-drafted 'How it was made' page from workshop logs, reviewed by the atelier head."

### 4.5 Content production at scale under brand guardrails
- Problem: need hundreds of assets (formats, markets, seasons) with a premium look.
- AI: generative image/video for backgrounds, scene variants and resizing (product shots stay photographed), brand-rules checker, copy variants within a tone library.
- KPI: asset throughput, cost per asset, creative test velocity, asset-level performance.
- Guardrail: never generate the jewellery itself (details wrong); label AI where required; human art director sign-off; see ai-disclosure-and-risk.md.
- Example: "One hero photo of an egg pendant -> 40 on-brand placements across Story/Reel/Display with AI-generated light and settings, retouched by the studio."

### 4.6 Localisation and cultural adaptation
- Problem: markets (Gulf, UK, US, Asia, Europe) need different tone, calendar and imagery.
- AI: translation + transcreation with a brand glossary, cultural-risk checker, local-event calendar suggestions.
- KPI: market-level conversion, time to launch in a new market, brand-consistency score.
- Guardrail: native reviewer; avoid stereotypes; religious sensitivities (Gulf, Eid).
- Example: "An Eid gifting campaign adapted in Arabic with local reviewers in hours, not weeks."

### 4.7 Paid media optimisation for high-value, low-volume conversions
- Problem: only a few conversions per week; boutique sales invisible to ad platforms.
- AI: value-based bidding (tROAS/maximise conversion value) with enhanced conversions and offline conversion import (CRM/boutique sale value, hashed email/phone), lead scoring (qualified enquiry vs junk), conversion-value rules by tier.
- KPI: cost per qualified appointment, value per lead, % of leads qualified, incremental revenue.
- Guardrail: consent, hashed data, no discount signalling; test against manual bidding.
- Example: "Boutique sales uploaded weekly so Google learns that a GBP 40k appointment equals 40x the value of a GBP 1k web order."

### 4.8 Audience modelling of HNWIs
- Problem: wealth cannot be targeted directly; broad lookalikes dilute.
- AI: look-alike/propensity models on first-party client data, clustering of client segments (collector, gifter, bridal, Gulf majlis), affluence-proxy scoring (geo, devices, travel), synthetic-persona testing for creatives.
- KPI: share of reach in target affluence bands, qualified-lead rate, CAC for qualified clients.
- Guardrail: privacy law, no sensitive-attribute inference, aggregated modelling only.
- Example: "Seed Meta/Google with top-5% value clients, exclude existing clients, add geo/placement filters."

### 4.9 Social listening and cultural trend detection
- Problem: the brand must read culture (royal weddings, auctions, film) without chasing trends.
- AI: LLM-based topic and sentiment tracking across press, social, auction results, red carpet; early alerts (e.g. a celebrity wears an egg pendant); share-of-voice vs peers (Cartier, Bulgari, Van Cleef, Boucheron, Graff).
- KPI: sentiment, share of voice, earned media value, reaction time.
- Guardrail: monitoring only public data; humans decide response.
- Example: "Alert when the Winter Egg or Romanov exhibition trends, with suggested editorial angles."

### 4.10 Search and GEO (generative engine optimisation)
- Problem: queries like "what is a Fabergé egg", "best egg pendant gift" are answered by AI overviews/ChatGPT/Perplexity; the brand story may be misrepresented or replaced by replicas and "Fabergé-style" sellers.
- AI: audit how LLMs describe Fabergé, structured data (schema.org Product/Organization), entity-rich heritage content, FAQ content, citations from authoritative sources (museums, auction houses), answer-engine monitoring.
- KPI: share of AI answers mentioning/linking Fabergé correctly, branded search volume, organic enquiry rate.
- Guardrail: factual accuracy; avoid spam content; fix wrong claims at the source.
- Example: "Monthly audit across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity for 50 target questions; fix gaps with a canonical heritage hub."

### 4.11 Virtual try-on / AR
- Problem: customers cannot see scale and effect of a pendant or ring remotely.
- AI: try-on with hand/neck tracking and accurate lighting; AI sizing from photos.
- KPI: conversion on try-on users, return rate (physical), enquiry rate.
- Guardrail: realism and scale accuracy (a bad try-on harms brand); no body-image issues; data deletion.
- Example: "See the egg pendant at real scale on your neck, then book an appointment to try the real piece."

### 4.12 Appointment-booking funnel
- Problem: drop-off between interest and a boutique/virtual appointment; slow replies.
- AI: conversational booking assistant, instant qualification, calendar integration, reminders, multilingual; routes high-value leads to senior advisor.
- KPI: appointment rate, show rate, speed to response, qualified appointment cost.
- Guardrail: human confirmation; privacy; do not collect sensitive wealth data.
- Example: "Chat widget books a private viewing in 3 questions; the advisor gets a 5-line briefing."

### 4.13 Measurement: MMM for low-volume luxury and incrementality
- Problem: last-click misleads; few conversions break standard models.
- AI: Bayesian MMM (Meridian, Robyn, PyMC-Marketing) using weekly sales, brand search, boutique footfall; geo-holdout tests; synthetic control; LLM assistant explaining results.
- KPI: incremental revenue, marginal ROI per channel, reduced wasted spend.
- Guardrail: limited data, wide intervals; combine with brand-lift and expert judgement.
- Example: "Geo test: pause Meta retargeting in 3 UK regions for 6 weeks, read the effect on boutique appointments and branded search."

### 4.14 Anti-counterfeit and brand-misuse monitoring
- Problem: "Fabergé" is a magnet for replicas, grey market and misleading "Fabergé-style" sellers; erodes trust and search share.
- AI: image recognition and LLM classification of marketplaces/social/ads; auto-flag listings and ad copy; takedown workflow; paid-search brand monitoring.
- KPI: listings removed, share of branded search results owned by official sites, customer complaints about fakes.
- Guardrail: legal review; human approves takedowns; avoid false positives against legit antique dealers.
- Example: "Weekly scan of 5 marketplaces flags 'Fabergé style egg' listings with image similarity > 0.8 for legal review."

## 5. Shortlist for a timed final (suggested)
Pick ONE part, not a platform; the brief says "one part of Fabergé's marketing". Strongest combos for a performance consultant:
1. 4.7 + 4.12 (qualified-appointment funnel + value-based bidding with offline conversions): measurable, performance-credible, luxury-safe. Best fit for Bjorn's profile.
2. 4.2 (gifting concierge) for a seasonal hook (Christmas / Easter).
3. 4.10 (GEO) for a differentiated, topical angle.
Decision rule: choose the one where (a) a 30/60/90 pilot is credible, (b) the KPI can be incremental-tested, (c) the guardrail story is simple.

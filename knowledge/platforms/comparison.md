## 2. Which platform for which goal (decision list)

- Capture existing high-intent demand (people already searching): Google Search (brand + non-brand), Shopping, Amazon Sponsored Products.
- Create demand / mass reach at low CPM: Meta (Reels), TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat (young), CTV (premium reach).
- App installs, volume: Google App campaigns (Android/Play) + Meta Advantage+ App + TikTok App promotion (+ Snapchat in young/Gulf markets).
- App events / deep funnel (first ride, first purchase, deposit): Meta and Google optimised on the deep event with enough volume; TikTok if creative strong; compare cost per deep event, not CPI.
- E-commerce purchases: Meta Advantage+ sales, Google PMax/Shopping/Search, TikTok Shop/Smart+, Pinterest (planners), Amazon (marketplace sellers).
- Lead gen B2C: Meta instant forms/WhatsApp, Google Search, TikTok Lead Gen.
- Lead gen B2B: LinkedIn (plus Google Search for intent), Reddit/X for niche tech.
- Local/foot traffic: Google (Maps/Local), Meta (local awareness), DOOH, Snapchat Promote Place.
- Brand awareness and lift: YouTube/CTV, Meta Reels, TikTok, DOOH; measure with brand lift.
- Retargeting: Meta dynamic ads, Google Display/Demand Gen/PMax, LinkedIn matched audiences, Pinterest, Amazon SD.
- Younger (13-24) reach: TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram, YouTube Shorts.
- Professionals/executives: LinkedIn, YouTube, newsletters/programmatic via contextual.
- Female planners/home/fashion: Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok.
- Gaming/tech enthusiasts: Reddit, Twitch (via Amazon DSP), YouTube, TikTok.
- Emerging-market mobile-first reach (e.g. Nigeria, India, Indonesia): Meta (incl. WhatsApp), TikTok, YouTube/Google App campaigns, X for cultural moments; check local penetration of Snapchat/Pinterest/LinkedIn/Reddit [CHECK].
- Building trust in a new market: influencer/creators (whitelisted) + local PR + community.
- Sales proof for retail brands: retail media (closed-loop).

Budget split heuristic for a performance case (adapt to case data; not a rule): 
- Start ~70-80% on 2-3 proven platforms (e.g. Meta + Google + TikTok), ~10-20% on testing, ~5-10% retargeting/brand defence.
- Shift budget by marginal cost per business action (CPA at the deep event) after 7-10 days; keep a floor on each platform to preserve learning.
- Cap any platform where marginal CPA rises sharply as spend scales (diminishing returns).

## 3. How the platforms differ

### 3.1 Algorithm-led broad targeting vs keyword intent vs B2B firmographics
- Algorithm-led (Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest partly): you supply creative + conversion signal + budget; the algorithm finds converters. Targeting levers shrink; creative and signal quality are the levers. Strength: scale and cost efficiency at the top/mid funnel. Weakness: volume depends on creative and event density; opaque.
- Keyword/query intent (Google Search, Shopping, Amazon): user states intent; you bid to appear. Strength: highest conversion rate, controllable. Weakness: demand ceiling (cannot exceed search volume); costs rise with competition.
- Hybrid automation (PMax, Demand Gen, App campaigns, AI Max): Google blends intent signals with algorithmic matching; less control, more reach.
- B2B firmographics (LinkedIn): target by job/company; high CPM but precise. Volume small, value per lead high.
- Contextual/curated (programmatic, Reddit communities): target environment rather than person; privacy-resilient, lower precision.
- Creator-led: audience trust borrowed from a person; creative and distribution fused.

### 3.2 View-through vs click attribution
- Click attribution is conservative and easy to audit. View-through counts conversions after an impression with no click; it is plausible for video/CTV/awareness but also captures people who would have converted anyway.
- Windows differ: Meta 7d click + 1d view, TikTok 7d click + 1d view, Snapchat 28d swipe + 1d view, LinkedIn 30d click + 7d view, Pinterest 30d click + 30d engagement + 1d view, Google Search 30d click, YouTube EVC 3d, Amazon SP 7d / SB-SD 14d [CHECK all].
- Consequences: a platform with a longer window or view credit reports more conversions for the same spend. Compare on a normalised basis (same window, click-only, or backend/MMP data).
- Multiple platforms claim the same conversion (double counting). Sum of platform-reported conversions usually exceeds actual business conversions. Reconcile with MMP/backend or use marketing mix modeling, geo-lift or holdouts.
- Modelled conversions (Meta, Google Consent Mode, iOS ATT/SKAN) fill gaps; accuracy varies.

### 3.3 Why the same KPI is not comparable across platforms
- CTR: a click on Meta Reels, a Google Search ad and a LinkedIn sponsored post are different behaviours; Search CTR of 5% and Meta CTR of 1% are both "normal".
- CPM: reflects audience value and competition (LinkedIn $50 vs TikTok $5); not quality per se.
- CPI / CPA: depends on the definition of install/conversion, attribution window, bidding objective (install-optimised campaigns get cheap installs that convert less), geography, device, and deduplication.
- ROAS: platform-reported ROAS varies with window, view-through, brand search inclusion and modelled data.
- IPM (installs per 1000 impressions) is creative-and-placement-specific; high IPM does not predict downstream rides/purchases.
- Engagement/video views: different counting thresholds (TikTok 2 s, Meta 3 s, YouTube 30 s/CPV, LinkedIn 2 s with 50% visible) [CHECK].
- Frequency/reach: not deduplicated across platforms; only a central ad server/panel gives cross-platform reach.
- Therefore: choose one north-star KPI in the case (e.g. cost per first ride from backend/MMP), and compare platforms on it using the same attribution window; use platform metrics only as diagnostics.

## 4. Case pattern: app install judged on CPA of first deep action (e.g. inDrive Nigeria, first ride)

- Define: KPI = cost per first completed ride (not CPI). Install-to-registration-to-first-ride funnel; compute implied CPI target = CPA target x install-to-first-ride rate.
- Android-heavy market: Google App campaigns (Play/Search/YouTube) and Meta Advantage+ App; TikTok for young urban reach; X/WhatsApp for local salience; Snapchat only if penetration confirmed [CHECK].
- Optimisation: begin on install or registration if first-ride volume is < 50/week/ad set, then migrate to the deep event; use event value if available.
- Tracking: MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust), Firebase, CAPI/App Events API, SKAN for iOS (small share); test event firing and postbacks before launch.
- Creative: local language and slang, Lagos/Abuja/Port Harcourt context, price-negotiation value prop (if inDrive-style), safety and reliability proof, driver/rider UGC, short vertical video with app UI.
- Two-sided marketplace: rider acquisition and driver supply may need separate campaigns/KPIs; check which side the case judges [CHECK case brief].
- Budget split logic: start broad across Meta/Google/TikTok, then reallocate by cost per first ride after 7-10 days; keep a test slice.
- Guardrails: fraud (click injection, bots, incentivised installs), install-to-ride rate, D1/D7 retention, promo abuse.
- Diagnostics vs KPIs: CTR, CPI and IPM are diagnostics; CPA first ride is the KPI.

What makes a movie successful?

Two things make a movie measurably successful — money and evidence — and they rarely coincide. Of 84,510 listings, 13.2% can support a money claim, 10.1% clear 100 votes, only 5.4% clear both. One film leads on both: The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King.

## 4 · New observations, round 2

### 4.1 Analytical Quality — 8.5 / 10

**Where it hits:** the ledger shows a hurdle-rate test and an NPV test side by side for all ten moves. The plan splits the
$60M into tranches with a numeric trigger: distribution cost down ≥ 0.6 pt and the airports still above 90 % in 6 of 12
months. The renovation case cites in-data evidence (seven hotels renovated in 2021 held NPS near +6 while the brand fell to
+2.7). "The data does not price the cost of waiting, so this case is understated" is the kind of self-aware caveat judges
reward. Behind the screen: 766 number checks, 0 failed (doc 23).

| ID | Sev. | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| J-AQ-7 | **Major** | **The Status column follows no single visible rule.** Breakfast (19.8 %), Energy (11.8 %), Kingston (8.2 %) and Boston (8.1 %) clear the 8 % line and are "not now"; the refresh (7.9 %, under the line) gets a $29.5M tranche. Breakfast, Kingston and the refresh are explained in What waits. **Energy ($15M, answers F1) and Boston ($24.8M, growth) are explained nowhere I can find**, and the plan's "Not needed · $8.7M" adds a fourth bucket. A finance-minded judge will read the ledger as a rule and find exceptions |
| J-AQ-8 | **Major** | **A has two "end numbers" on the Recommended view that don't reconcile on screen.** The tile says NPV through 2032 +$3.3M (Low −$5.5M, High +$12.7M). The plan chart ends at Expected +$6.8M, Low −$4.6M, High +$19.2M. The same happens in What waits: C is −$23.4M in the table and −$19.2M on the chart. The chart has no title saying it is cumulative and undiscounted, so the reader can't tell which figure is "the" result |
| J-AQ-9 | Minor | **"Guest score +0.25 there (dataset units)".** The unit is undefined. P1 shows NPS on −100 to +100, so +0.25 looks negligible, or like a different scale. "(dataset units)" reads as a working note |
| J-AQ-10 | Minor | **"A year on cost" is a 2032 yield set against a discount rate.** 2032 operating profit ÷ capex ignores ramp-up timing and asset life, so it is a screening heuristic, not a return. The caption defines it honestly; a finance judge may still challenge "two moves pass both tests" |
| J-AQ-11 | Minor | **Two renovation statements look contradictory across pages:** P1 says "None has started since 2020"; What waits cites "seven hotels renovated in 2021". The data agrees (works started in 2020 and finished in 2021), but the wording makes a reader stop |
